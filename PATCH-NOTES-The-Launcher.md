# Patch Notes: The Launcher

## The desktop app opens on a launcher
- **Daggerfall Online now opens on a launcher**: the patch notes on the left, your options on the right, and Play at the bottom. Nothing starts until you press Play.
- **Updates install before you play.** When a new version is out, the launcher downloads it, installs it and reopens by itself, so you play on the same version as everyone online. In a hurry? **Play without updating**, and it installs when you quit.
- **The patch notes are right there.** Versions that arrived since you last played are marked **New**; one on its way is marked **Update**.
- **Your options, beside the notes:** your game files (change the folder at any time), your saves folder, the update check (on or off) and Reinstall.
- The Windows installer and the Linux AppImage update themselves. The portable exe and the Mac tell you when a new version is out, with a Download button.

## Finding your Daggerfall files
- **On first run the launcher looks for Daggerfall itself**: Daggerfall Unity's settings, every Steam library, GOG, and a DaggerfallGameFiles.zip unpacked in Downloads, Desktop, Documents or Games. It shows what it finds, and you choose.
- **Choose the game's folder, not just ARENA2**, and it looks inside for you. Steam's "Browse local files" folder works.
- **A folder is only used when it is whole.** A folder missing some of the game's files says which ones.
- **If the folder you chose before cannot be reached** (a drive unplugged, a network share asleep), the launcher says so and offers Try again. It no longer freezes while it waits.
- **Choose in the game instead** is still there, one click away.
- On a Mac, the launcher points you to DaggerfallGameFiles.zip. Steam and GOG sell Daggerfall for Windows only.

## Downloads
- **The download buttons on the website always give you the newest version** for your system: the Windows installer, the Windows portable exe, the Mac app and the Linux AppImage.
- The app is not code-signed yet. On Windows choose More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac open it once, then choose Open Anyway under System Settings, Privacy & Security.
