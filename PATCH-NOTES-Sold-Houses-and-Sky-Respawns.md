# Patch Notes: sold houses, sky respawns and field fixes

## Houses
- **A house you brought online through customs can no longer be sold for nothing.** Selling it paid 0 gold and took the house and its furniture. The bank of the Empire does not buy back a house that came in through customs (as with a bought deed), so the door now says so and keeps it yours. A house you bought online still sells as before.
- **Pressing Sell twice no longer says "The account service had a problem."** The second press waits for the first sale's answer.

## Dying and respawning
- **You no longer respawn high in the air after dying in a dungeon.** You wake at the dungeon's own door, on the ground, instead of falling (and dying again) over a nearby town.

## Saving
- **A save taken while falling keeps the fall.** Loading it lands the fall's damage as it would have, instead of letting you land safely.

## Woodcutting and Foraging
- **Fixed the endless "Chop and Gather Wood..." lock.** Chopping wood twice quickly could hang the game when the wait ended, and every login after hung again. Characters already stuck recover at their next login once the wait finishes.

## Online characters
- **Creating an online character now starts the game.** It used to drop you back to the main menu after saving (the character was saved, and loaded fine from the Online door).
- **Bringing a character online says when it was played on another of your accounts.** If the realm knows the character from a different account (another browser, a guest account, the desktop app), it tells you to sign in with that account instead of saying it has no record.

## Guilds
- **Taking more gold from a guild treasury than you can carry pays it as a letter of credit.** A large withdrawal used to leave you too heavy to walk.

## Protect Bystanders
- **With Protect Bystanders on, your blows pass townspeople and the town's walking guards by during a fight.** A swing at an enemy just out of reach no longer lands on the townsperson behind it (no murder, no assault, no lost reputation). With no enemy near, attacking a townsperson is still a crime, as before.
