# Patch Notes: Rock fields back in the ground

## World of Daggerfall
- **No more giant dark shards hanging in the sky.** The last update's fix for floating bushes around bandit camps also lifted the big boulders that World of Daggerfall's rock fields are built from. They stuck up out of their rock piles as huge dark slabs, some hundreds of metres tall, around Hadus and near rock fields everywhere. The boulders are back where the mod sets them, down in the rock.
- The bushes around bandit camps still stand on the ground.
