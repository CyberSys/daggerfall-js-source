# Patch Notes: The hotbar by default

## Werewolves and wereboars
- **Your Lycanthropy spell is free from the hotbar**, just like it is when you cast it from the spellbook. Before, the
  hotbar (and the diamond's spell key) charged 5 spell points, and with an empty pool it refused with "You don't have
  the spell points."

## Quick slots
- **The hotbar is now on by default.** Ten slots sit above your health bars on keys 1 to 0. Fill them by dragging
  items from your pack and spells from your spellbook.
- **Prefer the diamond?** Set Features > Interface > Quick slots to Diamond. If you had turned quick slots Off, they
  stay off.
