# Patch Notes: Oblivion Gates - the Warden reforged

Fixes and changes after the first live fights, from your reports.

## Fixed
- **The gate and the Burning Court are visible.** The gate's horned stone, the court's floor, spires, braziers and bridge, and the islands in the fire were never being drawn (you were standing on an invisible floor). They are now.
- **You can leave after the fight.** When Valkynaz Ruhn falls, a portal of fire rises where he died. Walk through it (or press on it) to go home. The bridge's way home still works too.
- **Soul Trap works on him.** Cast it on him and kill him while it lasts: his soul goes into an empty soul gem.

## The fight
- **Three named phases, each with its own attacks** (not faster ones):
  - **The Warden**: Cleave, Ground Slam, Charge.
  - **The Burning Court**: Hellfire and the new **Meteor of Oblivion** leave the floor burning; staying in the fire hurts every second. Flame Nova, and the new **Crushing Leap** at anyone standing far away.
  - **Dagon's Champion**: the new **Spokes of Dagon**, four lanes of fire from his feet. Stand between them.
- At each phase change he leaps to the centre of the court and casts that phase's signature attack. The boss bar shows the phase's name.
- **A ring and an arrow under him** show where he stands and which way he faces.
- **He hits harder**: every attack takes a larger share of your health plus a flat amount. Two hits leave you low and a third will finish you; the Flame Nova and the Meteor take nearly half your health each, so two of those will finish you.
- **No regeneration in the Deadlands.** Regenerate spells and items, and a class's Regenerate Health, do nothing in the court. Healing spells and potions still work.
- **Your gear doesn't wear out on him.** Hitting the boss no longer damages your weapon.

## Spoils
- **Each drop shows its own picture** on the floor, with a small line of its rarity's colour rising from it.
- Your spoils are yours alone: nobody else can see or take them. Each piece can be picked up a moment after it lands, so you see it before you take it.

## The sky over a gate
- **The sky burns over an Oblivion Gate**, as it does in the Dread event: a crimson sky and light as soon as the gate is announced, growing deeper as it rises and opens, and clearing as it collapses. It's full over the whole area around the gate, including the town it's named after, and fades out about 12 km away.
- **A red storm gathers over the gate.** Its lightning strikes around the gate itself, so you can follow it there. The thunder is heard from wherever each strike lands.

## Music
- **The boss music is much louder and more intense.** It was too quiet to hear over the court. All four songs are now clearly louder than the dungeon music (and still never clip at full volume). The strings, brass and drums start from the first bar, and the rhythm drives harder with each phase.

## More fixes (from a full audit)
- Players who join and then leave no longer make the boss tougher for everyone who stays. In a full court, a seat is only held by someone who has dealt real damage or stood in the fight for 30 seconds.
- Standing at the very edge of the court no longer keeps you out of his Cleave's reach.
- The health bar keeps moving during the Wrath's wind-up, and hits after the Wrath lands no longer count.
- The spoils from a kill can no longer be collected twice on two devices or in two tabs. They roll at the level you fought at or your character's level, whichever is lower. If the game closes before a save, they're recovered safely.
- Loot on the floor is drawn for your own character, not a default one.
- Mehrunes' Razor and "Cast When Strikes" weapons no longer break or wear down on the boss, and Cast When Strikes spells now hit him.
- His body, your spoils and the portal home appear where he actually fell, even mid-charge or mid-leap.
- Soul Trap reaches him at range and as a burst. Recasting adds to the running trap, and the trap survives stepping out of the court and back in.
- The Charge is drawn exactly as wide as it hits.
- Stepping in and out of burning ground no longer dodges its damage.
- No footsteps while he's in the air mid-leap.
- If the gate rises while you stand in one of its horns, you're set down in front of it instead of trapped in the stone.
- The boss music plays even if the game's music archive failed to load. The victory fanfare is no longer cut short.
- A tab left open from before an update is told the gate is closed (reload to fight), instead of misjudging the new attacks.
- Spoils recovered after a crash are no longer handed back again every time you start the game offline.
- If everyone leaves the court and comes back later, the boss is still as wounded as they left him - he no longer returns at full health.
- Your spoils are no longer handed a second time to another device or tab of yours right after a kill.
- A boss killed in mid-leap now falls in the same spot on every screen, and hits on him mid-leap land where you see him.
