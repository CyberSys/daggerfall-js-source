# Patch Notes: Quest kills count for the whole party

## Quests (online)
- **A quest monster killed by a party member now counts for your quest, wherever you are.** When you share a quest with your party and then die or leave while its monster is still standing, the monster passes to the party member nearest it. If that member hadn't taken the quest themselves and killed the monster with nobody on the quest nearby, the kill counted for nobody, and the monster was back when you returned. Now the kill reaches your quest, and the quest of every party member who shares it, as it happens.
- **Your quest's monsters are yours.** Players outside your party can't see them, hit them or be attacked by them, so someone else clearing a dungeon never takes your quest's target. A dungeon's own monsters, a cave's tigers included, are everyone's, and come back an hour after they die.
