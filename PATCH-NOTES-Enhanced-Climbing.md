# Patch Notes: Enhanced Climbing

## Climb onto ledges
Climbing now knows what a ledge is. Face a ledge and press **Jump**, and you pull yourself up onto it instead of jumping.

- **From the ground:** a ledge up to about chest height at Climbing 0, and a little above your head at Climbing 100.
- **In the air:** keep **Jump** held after a jump or while falling, and you catch any ledge that comes within reach of your hands and climb up. A tap doesn't catch anything; you have to hold it.
- **Low ceilings:** if there's only room to crouch on top, you end up crouched.
- If there's no ledge in reach, Jump is an ordinary jump, the same as before.

## Vault low walls and fences
Run at a waist-high wall, fence or table and press **Jump** to vault over it and keep going. Standing still, the same press climbs onto anything wide enough to stand on.

## Climbing a wall ends at the top
Holding Forward against a wall still climbs it the classic way. When the top comes within reach, you now pull yourself over the edge instead of being pushed over it.

## Your skills matter
- **Climbing** sets how high you can reach and how fast you pull yourself up. At Climbing 100 a head-high ledge takes about half a second.
- Each climb onto a ledge costs about as much fatigue as a jump and trains **Climbing**. A vault trains **Jumping**.
- Khajiit and the Climbing spell help here too.

## Where to find it
**Enhanced climbing** is under *The world* in Features. It's on by default. Turn it off to keep Daggerfall's own climbing. Offline, the classic UI always uses classic climbing. Online it's on for everyone, whichever UI they use, so every player can reach the same places.
