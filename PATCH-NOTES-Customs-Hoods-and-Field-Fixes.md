# Patch Notes: Customs, hoods and field fixes

## Bringing a character online
- **Bring online** accepts every character the realm knew before it opened: one that earned Renown, owned an online home, held a guild place, fought a raid, or was backed up to your account before the update. Before, only characters that had earned Renown online could come in.
- Your **online homes and your guild place come with the character**. If you already brought a character in and lost them, they are back.
- **Bring online now asks first.** It shows what customs will do: the loans it calls in, the gold over your level's allowance that stays behind, and any deeds that stay. Your offline character keeps everything, loans and gold included, and keeps playing offline.
- The refusal now says what counts, instead of "Only a character that has already played online".

## Vampires
- **A vampire with a hood up can travel by day.** Wear a cloak or plain robes with the hood raised (click it on your character) and the Travel Map opens in daylight, online and offline. Bare-headed, you are told how.
- Your stats are still 20 lower by day.

## Shops
- **Nothing sells for 0 gold.** Every piece a shop sells or repairs costs at least 1 gold. Selling to a shop is unchanged.

## Raids
- With **Protect Bystanders** on, your melee swings no longer hit the town's defenders during a raid, so defending a town no longer gets you arrested. A defender standing in front of you stops the swing.

## The Overworld
- **Picking a far destination on the Travel Map no longer drops the frame rate.** The route line is only drawn where it is on screen.
- **The mouse is free on the Overworld** from the first moment. Starting or resuming a journey from the map no longer traps it until you press Escape.

## Items
- The **Mark of Featherweight** reads "Cast when used: Slowfalling", not "ERROR". It always worked.
