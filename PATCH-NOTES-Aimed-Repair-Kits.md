# Patch Notes: Aimed Repair Kits

## Choose what a repair kit mends
Using a Repair Kit or a Field Repair Kit from your inventory now asks **which piece to mend** whenever more than one piece could use it. You no longer have to drop your loot to get a kit onto the sword you care about.

- **Classic inventory:** a list pops up with each weapon or armour piece and how worn it is, for example `Iron Longsword 42% (worn)`. Double-click a row or pick it with the arrow keys and Enter. Click outside the list to keep the kit.
- **Enhanced inventory:** a "Mend which?" window lists the same pieces. Click one to mend it, or press **Keep** (or Escape) to keep the kit.
- The pieces you're wearing come first, then the rest of your pack, each group most worn first. `(worn)` marks the ones you have on.
- If only one piece needs mending, the kit mends it straight away, the same as before.

## Smarter mending from the hotbar
A kit used from a hotbar or quickslot can't ask, so it now mends **what you're wearing first**, then the pack, instead of whatever happened to be most worn.

## Fixes
- **Kits no longer mend arrows.** Arrows always counted as the most worn weapon, so Field Repair Kits and Iron Repair Kits were spent on the quiver.
- A kit used from a corpse or a chest now mends your own gear, not the other items in that pile.
