# Patch Notes: Professions - Mining, Quarrying and the Forge

## Mining (online)
- Online, **ore veins** now stand in the wilderness, at the foot of the rocks in rock fields. Where a place has no rocks, a vein sits on bare stone nearby. Every player sees the same veins, and they change each day.
- Walk up to a vein and the prompt tells you what it holds and what **E** will do. You need a **Pick-Axe** (Foraging's) and enough Mining for the metal.
- Press **E** to start mining. Five points on the rock take turns to **glint**. **Attack** to strike: one strike per swing, and a strike aimed at the glinting point counts **double**. The glint moves after every strike, and on its own after a moment (longer at Master rank, and longer with good Intelligence and Agility).
- A vein takes 4, 5 or 7 strikes by its tier. If every strike lands on the glint, it is a **clean** finish, worth half as much XP again. Press Escape at any time and nothing is lost.
- Each strike on the glint has a small chance to turn up a **gem** beside the ore: Amber, Jade, Turquoise, Malachite, or a Ruby, Sapphire or Emerald in the mountains. At most one a vein.
- **Boulders** in rock fields can be quarried for **Rough Stone**. A clean finish cuts it at the rock into **Cut Stone**, two for one.
- **Dungeons** hold one to four veins a day on their walls, deeper metals than the surface: Silver, Gold, Dwarven Scrap, Platinum, Adamantium, and Moonstone in woodland dungeons. A dungeon vein can give a **Diamond**. Mining underground works at any hour.
- You can mine **60** times a day, a count of its own apart from Herbalism's, and on the surface only in daylight.

## The kingdoms' signatures
- Where players have confirmed the land, each kingdom's first vein on a map square is its own ore: **Daggerfall's Moonstone** (its first two), **Wayrest's Mithril**, **Sentinel's Ebony**. The Orsinium Area and the Wrothgarian Mountains give **Orichalcum**, and the Isle of Balfiera **Adamantium**.
- Land and dungeons are confirmed when three players who have been registered for a week have worked them. Until then they give only their lower tiers, and no gems.

## The Forge
- The **Stores page** has a **Forge** section. Stand inside a **Weaponsmith's** or an **Armorer's** (50 gold a smelt) or your own home with a **Forge** station, and smelt from your Stores:
  - two of a raw metal or ore make one **ingot** (Iron, Silver, Moonstone, Dwarven, Mithril, Adamantium, Ebony, Orichalcum);
  - one Copper and one Tin make **Brass**;
  - one Iron Ingot and one **Charcoal** make **Steel** (Charcoal comes with Logging, later).
- Up to 100 at a time. Smelting trains **Smithing**.
- In a home, a placed piece can be made a **Forge** for 50,000 gold, like the other crafting stations.

## Items
- Withdrawn metals and gems are Daggerfall's own items. The new ores, the ingots and the stone are new items drawn with Daggerfall's own pictures, coloured by their metal the way Daggerfall colours an Ebony blade.

## Specialisations
- **Prospector** (rank 50): veins within 200 m show on the compass, and gems come a little more often.
- **Deep Delver** (rank 50): dungeon veins give half as much again.
- **Stonebreaker** (rank 100): quarrying always gives Cut Stone.

## Court Writs
- Court writs now ask for metal and stone as well as herbs.

## Fixes
- Escape now ends a gathering act in a dungeon too.
- A harvest's message could appear twice when the server was slow; it now appears once.

## Rollout
- The professions open to the developers first. Everyone else's game is unchanged until they are switched on for all.
- Offline, nothing changes.
