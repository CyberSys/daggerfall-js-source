# Patch Notes: Terms of Service and Privacy Policy

## Accounts
- Daggerfall Online now has a Terms of Service and a Privacy Policy, at daggerfalljs.dev/terms/ and daggerfalljs.dev/privacy/. Both are linked at the bottom of the home page.
- Creating an account now asks you to agree to both. Two boxes sit under the password, each beside a link to its document. The link opens in a new tab (in the desktop app, in your browser), so nothing you typed is lost while you read.
- The account is only created once both boxes are ticked. Giving a guest account a username asks the same.
- Accounts that already exist are not asked. Signing in and recovering a password work as before.

## If creating an account fails
- If it says the game may need updating, reload the game (in the desktop app, update the app): the version you have open is from before this update.
- If it says the documents have changed, reload the game (or update the desktop app) to read the current versions, then tick the boxes again.
- While you type or tick a box on the form, the cursor stays where it was when a message clears.
