# Patch Notes: Silver

## The currency has a new name
- **Drakes are now called silver**, everywhere you see them: your account card, the Bank ("Sell silver", "8 gold for
  each silver"), the Market and Work tabs, writs, the professions' prices, the Seat tab and your guild's **silver
  treasury**.
- The metal is unchanged: **Silver** ore and the **Silver Ingot** are still materials, as before.
- **Nothing else changed.** Your balance is exactly what it was, just under the new name.
