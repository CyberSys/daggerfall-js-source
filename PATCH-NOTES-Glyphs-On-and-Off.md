# Patch Notes: Glyphs On and Off

## Glyphs
- **You can now hide your glyphs.** Press a glyph on the account card to hide it, and press it again to show it. Other players see the change the next time you enter a new place.
- Hiding a glyph only hides the icon. Anything the glyph lets you do still works.
- tabbyvish is now a moderator, with the blue shield and `/mute` and `/unmute`.
