# Patch Notes: What a vein needs

## Professions
- **Pressing E at a vein, tree or herb you can't work yet now tells you why.** Before, E did nothing at all when your rank was too low, you had no tool, or you had reached the day's limit. Now, if nothing else is there to use, you get a message such as "Mine Silver: needs Mining 25 - your Mining is 0". A door, chest or enemy right beside the node still gets your E press first, as before.

## Good to know
- **Every dungeon vein needs Mining 25 or more.** A dungeon nobody has confirmed yet holds only Silver, which needs Mining 25. Raise Mining at the ore veins in the wilderness rock fields first. They can be mined by day.
- **Using the Pick-Axe from your inventory or hotbar is Foraging's mining, not a vein's.** It only works in the wilderness, so in a dungeon it says "You cannot mine in here!". To mine a vein, carry a Pick-Axe, look at the vein and press **E**.
