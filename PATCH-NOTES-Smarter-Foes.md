# Patch Notes: Smarter Foes

## No more door griefing (always on)
- **Foes no longer stand in doorways.** Guards and enemies step off a door's threshold and out of its mouth, so a crowd can't seal a building or a dungeon door.
- **Clicking a door works even with a guard in front of it.** If you're aiming at a door, a peaceful guard standing in the way no longer eats the click. Hostile foes still take it, so you can always swing at someone attacking you.
- **The indoor watch spawns inside, spread out.** Guards called into an interior appear past the threshold and spaced apart, instead of stacked on the door.
- **Foes are aware of each other.** Guards, bandits, dungeon foes and wilderness encounters now push apart across groups, so mixed crowds no longer stand inside one another.

## Cover (Enhanced AI)
- **Trees, crates, barrels, statues and solid decor block sight.** A foe can't see you through a tree trunk or a stack of crates.
- **They also stop missiles, both ways.** Your arrows and bolts hit the cover, and so do theirs. Area spells still burst where they land.
- Grass, flowers, small clutter and see-through props aren't cover. Felled trees stop being cover.

## Tactics (Enhanced AI)
- **Attack turns.** At most two melee foes and two ranged foes attack you at once. The rest hold a ring around you, circle and wait their turn.
- **Knowing when to strike.** Melee foes step in when you're open (after your swing, while casting, drinking, turned away or staggered) and back out after their own blow. A patience clock means nobody waits forever.
- **Backing off.** A hurt foe gives ground and circles before coming back.
- **Fleeing.** Animals and cowardly people run at low health. Guards, undead, daedra and constructs never do.
- **Archers and casters keep their distance.** They back away from a closing melee fighter, fight when cornered, and look for a clear shot instead of firing into a crate.
- Applies everywhere (dungeons, streets, interiors and guards) when **Enhanced AI** is on. With it off, foes behave exactly as in classic Daggerfall.

## Telegraphed attacks (Enhanced AI)
- **Tougher foes have a special attack with a wind-up**, like the world boss's but smaller: level 10 and up, or elite foes.
- **Three shapes, one or two per kind of foe:**
  - **Lunge:** a long lane ahead (beasts, swordsmen).
  - **Sweep:** a wide cone in front (brutes, blades).
  - **Slam:** a disc just ahead (giants, atronachs, daedra lords).
- **Readable and dodgeable.** The shape glows dim on the ground through a short wind-up (0.7 to 0.9 s) and flashes when it lands. Step out of it and it misses. A hit does 1.25x to 1.75x a normal blow.
- **Used sparingly.** Each foe has a cooldown of 8 to 15 seconds, and only one foe near you winds up at a time. Casters, spirits, small and flying foes don't use them.
- The ground marks follow slopes and fade into fog.
