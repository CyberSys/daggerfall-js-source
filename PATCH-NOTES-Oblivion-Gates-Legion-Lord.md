# Patch Notes: Oblivion Gates - the Legion-Lord

The Warden can now bring his host. A new trial, **Legion-Lord**, joins the nightly rotation, and on those nights Daedra fight beside him.

## A ninth trial
- **Legion-Lord** joins the eight trials the Warden can come with. Like the others it shows on the marks' card, under his health bar, in the omen's chat line, on the map's card and on Discord.
- The rotation is rebuilt for nine trials: every aspect still meets every pair of trials once, now over 144 gates (twelve days). No two gates in a row share a mark, and every trial comes up as often as the rest.
- Tonight's marks have moved: the gates to come are the new rotation's, not the old one's.

## His host
- **Imps** (first court): a few rise out of the fire at the rim every half minute or so. Each one runs at whoever stands **farthest from the Warden** and bites. The bite is marked on the floor, so step out of it. Melee fighters can pick them off before they reach the archers and casters at the edge.
- **Atronachs** (second court): once the Burning Court's turn is done, Atronachs of his element (Fire, Ice, Iron or Flesh) rise on the far side of the court and **walk straight at the Warden**, their path faint on the floor. **Kill them before they reach him**: one that gets there is drunk, and he heals a little. They don't attack.
- **Ward-Bearers** (second and third courts): Daedra rise around the heart of each new court as he lands there. **His ward holds while any of them stand**, for up to 25 seconds; break the last one and his ward breaks. They pulse at anyone who comes close, in his element; step out of the ring they mark.
- Every one of them rises for a moment before it moves or strikes. At each phase change they crumble, and they go with him when he falls or Dagon's Wrath comes.
- Blows on his host count toward your part in the fight (and your spoils) like blows on him. The damage chart gets a **Host** column on Legion-Lord nights.

## On screen
- While his Ward-Bearers hold his ward, the bar counts them ("Ward-Bearers - 2 of 3 stand"), and the rest of his host is counted under it.
- Each wave, each Atronach he drinks and each Ward-Bearer cut down is said as it happens.

Reload the game after the update to fight (the gate needs this version).
