# Patch Notes: Bloodmoon's Werewolf, and Shadow Fang

## The werewolf, in Morrowind 3D
- With Morrowind assets on and Bloodmoon attached (Bloodmoon.bsa and Bloodmoon.esm), a transformed werewolf is now Bloodmoon's own werewolf model, in first and third person, built from your Bloodmoon files by OpenMW's rules. It has not yet been seen on real Bloodmoon data: if yours will not build, the Morrowind card names the file it stopped at.
- Other players see your werewolf the same way if they have Bloodmoon attached too. While their copy of your wolf builds, they see the Eye Of The Beholder werewolf sprite.
- Without Bloodmoon nothing changes: the werewolf is still the Eye Of The Beholder sprite and the classic claws, and turning back brings your Morrowind body back.
- Turning back brings back your own body and gear. The werewolf holds no weapon, torch or lantern, and casts no spell.
- The wereboar has no Morrowind model and is unchanged.

## Titles and glyphs
- New title: **Shadow Fang**, in a black-to-crimson gradient edged in crimson, with a snarling wolf's-head glyph. It is SirMcMobdon's.
- A Shadow Fang werewolf wears its own skin: blacker fur and crimson through the fur's edges. Its eyes burn red when Bloodmoon's texture names them as eyes or draws them as glowing eyes on the head. Anyone who has Bloodmoon attached sees the skin.
- Tabby is now a Developer. Flylighter is now a Disciple. SirMcMobdon is no longer an Apostle, and holds Shadow Fang: press it on the account card to wear it.

## Notes
- This update redeploys the online relay: connected players are dropped once and reconnect.
- The account service now waits for the relay to be updated before it deploys, so a new title never reaches the relay before the relay knows it.
