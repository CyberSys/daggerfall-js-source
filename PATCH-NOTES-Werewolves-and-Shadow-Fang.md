# Patch Notes: Bloodmoon's Werewolf, and Shadow Fang

## The werewolf, in Morrowind 3D
- With Morrowind assets on and Bloodmoon attached (Bloodmoon.bsa and Bloodmoon.esm), a transformed werewolf is now Bloodmoon's own werewolf model, in first and third person, built the way OpenMW builds it.
- Other players see your werewolf the same way if they have Bloodmoon attached too.
- Without Bloodmoon nothing changes: the werewolf is still the Eye Of The Beholder sprite and the classic claws.
- Turning back brings back your own body and gear. The werewolf holds no weapon, torch or lantern.
- The wereboar has no Morrowind model and is unchanged.

## Titles and glyphs
- New title: **Shadow Fang**, in a black-to-crimson gradient, with a snarling wolf's-head glyph. It is SirMcMobdon's.
- A Shadow Fang werewolf wears its own skin: blacker fur, crimson through the fur's edges, red eyes. Everyone with Bloodmoon attached sees it.
- Tabby is now a Developer. Flylighter is now a Disciple. SirMcMobdon moves from Apostle to Shadow Fang.

## Notes
- This update redeploys the online relay: connected players are dropped once and reconnect.
