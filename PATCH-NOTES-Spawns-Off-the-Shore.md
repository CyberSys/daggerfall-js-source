# Patch Notes: Spawned dungeons stay off the shore

## Fixes
- Spawned dungeons (normal and Elite) no longer appear on beaches or out in the sea. Before, a dungeon could stand on a square of sand jutting into the water off a coast.
- A spawned dungeon now only appears where its ground is dry land, above the beach line. Coastal land well above the water still gets dungeons.
- The Overworld no longer marks a far spawned dungeon at the water's edge that was never really there.

## Notes
- Every player still sees the same dungeons in the same places. Pixels that used to hold a shoreline spawn are now empty land (or sea) for everyone.
- Offline play is unaffected (spawned dungeons are online only).
