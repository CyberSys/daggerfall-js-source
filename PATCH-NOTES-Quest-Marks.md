# Patch Notes: Quest marks

## Quests (Enhanced interface)
- **Your quests on the map.** Open the map and every active quest's destination is marked with a gold diamond above the place. The quest you're following is filled in; the others are outlined. Point at one to see the quest, where it is, and how long you have left.
- **And on the compass.** When you're outdoors, a gold diamond on the compass shows which way to go for the quest you're following.
- **Only what you already know.** A place is only marked once it's on your map, the same rule Daggerfall's own logbook uses before it offers to show you a place. Nothing is revealed early.
- **Not on your map yet?** The quest card on your HUD says so and tells you to ask around. Townsfolk can give you directions and mark it for you.
- Turn it off under Features, Interface, "Quest marks". The Classic interface, and Daggerfall's own map windows if you prefer them, are unchanged.
