# Patch Notes: What a harvest says

## Professions
- **You can always see what you gathered.** Some harvests used to show more messages than fit on screen, such as a gem, Resin, a body's part and meat, a new rank, or the specialisation reminder at rank 50 or 100. When that happened, the line saying what went to your Stores was the first one pushed off, so it looked as if you got nothing. Everything you gathered is now listed on one line, for example "+1 Bear Hide, a Big Tooth and 2 Raw Meat to your Stores", and that line stays on screen when other messages arrive.
- **Your first harvest each session says where your goods went.** Gathered goods go to your **Stores**, not your pack. You can find them on the Enhanced pause menu, on the Stores page.
- **Every harvest that is waiting to be counted says so.** Before, only the first slow harvest in a session showed a message. If you are signed out, it now tells you that the harvest is kept for ten minutes and counted once you sign in. If a harvest is never counted, the message says so plainly.
- **A gathering that stops early tells you.** If you let go, walk away, open a window or leave the dungeon before the end, you now see "The gathering stopped before its end - nothing was taken." Before, only the meter disappeared. Pressing Escape still ends it quietly.
- **A harvest made while the server is under maintenance is kept, not lost.** It is counted once maintenance is over.

## Good to know
- **XP and materials were always counted.** Only the on-screen messages went missing. Your ranks and Stores already hold everything you gathered.
