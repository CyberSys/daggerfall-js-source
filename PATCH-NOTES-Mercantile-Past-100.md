# Patch Notes: Mercantile past 100

## Shops (online)
- **Selling never costs you gold.** Enchantments and affixes can lift Mercantile past 100, and past it a shop's offer kept falling, until selling took gold from your purse. Online, shops now count any Mercantile or Personality past 100 as 100: a sale pays what it pays at 100, and never less.
- **Nothing is free past 100 either.** Far past 100, a shop sold its goods for a gold apiece, and rooms, cures and spells paid you to take them. Online they now cost what they cost a character at 100.
- Mercantile and Personality up to 100 haggle exactly as before, and offline nothing has changed.
