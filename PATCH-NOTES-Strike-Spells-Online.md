# Patch Notes: Strike spells online

## Online
- **"Cast When Strikes" weapons work on monsters another player spawned.** Before, only a strike spell's damage reached a monster someone else in your party or cell had spawned. Paralysis, sleep, drains, pacify and soul trap landed on your own copy of the monster and were gone a moment later. The whole spell now reaches the real monster, which still gets its saving throw as usual. A Hand of Sleep blade puts a shared foe to sleep, and the strike's damage counts as your blow.
- **Your soul trap fills your own gem.** A soul trap from your weapon on another player's monster is yours. When the monster falls, whoever lands the killing blow, its soul goes into an empty soul gem in your pack, not the other player's.

## Good to know
- Strike enchantments still do not fire in duels.
- When your strike puts a soul trap on another player's monster, you do not see "Trap active." You find out when it dies.
