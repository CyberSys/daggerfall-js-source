# Patch Notes: Mining, regeneration and the King's Mark

## Mining and the other professions
- **Either mouse button strikes.** Once you start mining with E, a left click now strikes the vein as well as a right click. The bar says so: "click to strike the glint". Chopping a tree works the same way.
- **Mining works on phones and controllers.** The Attack button, a swipe and the controller's attack never reached a mining act, so a vein could not be mined there. They now strike, as do taps during the act. Chopping, the Basket's search and the fishing net's tug take them too. Start the act with E, or by using the tool from your hotbar.
- **Boulders can be targeted by looking at their stones.** You had to aim about a metre up the rock's face to get the prompt. Looking at the glowing stones at its foot now works.
- **A hidden vein no longer blocks the one you can see.** A vein hidden behind its rock but nearest your crosshair stopped any other vein in view from being targeted.
- **No more veins inside rocks.** A vein could stand inside a rock, glowing and on the compass, but impossible to reach. Veins now always stand outside the rocks.
- **Walking up to a vein no longer climbs its rock.** Pressing forward against a rock started a climb after a moment, and the vein was lost. While any gathering spot (a vein, boulder, herb patch, tree, hunted body or fishing spot) is under your crosshair, or while you're gathering, pressing forward against a wall no longer starts a climb. Jumping still grabs the wall and climbs.

## Professions at any hour
- **The professions no longer close at night.** Mining, Quarrying, Herbalism, the Basket, Logging and Fishing used to refuse to start from 18:00 to 06:59 ("You need daylight to mine effectively!"), and the counting-house refused a harvest that finished after dark. You can now gather at any hour. Fish still bite a little faster in the first and last hour of daylight. (Offline, the Foraging tools keep their daylight rule.)

## More fixes to the professions
- **The last click no longer opens what's behind the node.** Finishing a vein, a tree or the Basket's search with a left click opened the door, chest, body or lever under your crosshair when you let go.
- **Phone and controller strikes.** Resting your thumb on the move stick, or tapping the docked bar, no longer strikes during an act. A quick double tap of Attack now strikes twice. On the Enhanced Plus controller layout, holding RT strikes once, no longer every 0.4 seconds, so a held trigger can't fell a tree or catch every tug by itself.
- **Trees are easier to target.** A tree can be targeted anywhere up its trunk to the top of its glow, from close up and from horseback. It used to answer only at the bottom 1.2 m of the trunk. Herb patches answer anywhere in their glow.
- **No herb patches or trees inside rocks.** Like veins, they now always stand outside the rocks.
- **Confirmed areas update right away.** When an area becomes confirmed (by your harvest or anyone's), its veins, trees and herbs show their real kinds at once. Before, they stayed the same until midnight UTC, so you could be offered a node, work it, and then hear "Your craft is not yet skilled enough for that." Confirmed areas and dungeons also no longer forget they're confirmed at midnight UTC.
- **"Stores full" counts everything.** Stores bought with gold now count toward the 5,000 limit before you start, as the counting-house counts them.
- **Daily limits are shown, not hit.** Once the counting-house says your account's day in a craft is done, or that you've mined your four veins in dungeons nobody has vouched for, the node's prompt says so instead of letting you work it and refusing it. If your character's day or your Stores filled up on another device, the professions check again.
- **No lost harvests from busy hours.** A harvest refused because you've done a great deal at your crafts this hour is now kept and sent again, instead of lost.
- **The smith sells Charcoal at the Forge.** You can buy it there and smelt Steel without Logging. The smith's and the furnisher's counters now check your Drakes before you buy, as the Weavers' counter does.
- **Controllers on the Classic UI** can now press the Stores page's buttons (Forge, Anvil, Workbench, Withdraw).
- **The steady hand says what to do.** Its meter now says "hold E and keep still".
- **Seasonal Eye takes effect at once** when you choose it.
- **At sea, E boards a struck ship** (or opens your prize) instead of casting your net.
- **Climbing after sign-out.** If the professions closed while a node was under your crosshair (after signing out, for example), climbing stayed blocked. It works again right away.

## Phones, controllers and Herbalism
- **An E button on phones.** The bottom-right corner now has a third button by default: E, the use key. Press it to pick a herb, cast the fishing net or open a door; hold it to keep the steady hand, draw the skinning line or raise the net's band. You can change or remove it on the Touch card, like the other two. The mode and F buttons sit one place further in.
- **E on controllers.** On the Classic UI, B is now the use key in the world (it is still Back in menus). On Enhanced Plus, LT is the use key; Recast spell, which LT did before, is now a hold of d-pad right (a tap still rests). You can rebind it in the Controller bindings window. With a controller in hand, the professions' prompts name its button ("[LT] Pick Red Rose") instead of E.
- **Use the Skinning Knife from your hotbar.** Put it on the hotbar or a quick slot and use it at a body you felled: it starts skinning and holds the knife for you, so you only aim at the first point and draw the line by looking - a swipe on a phone, the right stick on a controller. Away from a body it tells you where Hunting is done.
- **Herbalism XP keeps up with your rank.** Herbs only go up to the third tier, so past rank 70 every herb gave a quarter of its XP, and reaching Master took months. Each herb's XP now follows your rank, the way Fishing's does: at rank 70 a herb is worth 90 XP (135 picked unbruised), where it was 3 to 16. The Basket's food is unchanged.

## Spells
- **Area buffs now include you.** An Area Around Caster or Area at Range spell made only of buffs (for example Regenerate with Fortify Attribute) now lands on you as well as everyone it reaches, wherever it bursts. It lands in full: before, your own magic resistance rolled against it when it caught you, so many of its Regenerate rounds healed nothing. Area spells that also do harm work as before: they reach you only if you're inside the blast, and you can resist them.
- **Cured drains leave the HUD.** After a vampire's or werewolf's transformation, or a guild's attribute restoration, a drained attribute that was cured left a debuff icon that never went away. It's gone now, including from saves. The transformation also now fills your health, fatigue and magicka all the way, even when an attribute that raises them was drained.
- **Becoming a vampire or a werewolf no longer hides your running spells.** The transformation marked every spell effect on you as ended. A running Regenerate kept healing but vanished from the HUD, your party's cards and the dispel list, and recasting it never brought the icon back. Your buffs now keep running and stay shown. The transformation also cures your poisons and fully restores your health, fatigue and magicka, as Daggerfall's does.

## Items
- **Legendary jewellery and other tiered items no longer come out as wands.** One jewel in eight from the Oblivion Gate's spoils and a town's thanks was a wand. A wand can't be worn, so its powers never worked; a King's Mark on one could never be equipped. They now come out as wearable jewellery. Any tiered wand you already have (Magic, Rare or Legendary) becomes an amulet when you load, keeping its powers; a Legendary keeps its name too. Ordinary and Daggerfall's own magic wands stay wands.
- **Change how your cloak is worn again.** Since hoods got their own button, a worn casual cloak could no longer change its drape on the Enhanced Plus inventory. Its card now has **Change drape** beside **Raise hood**. It moves to the next way of wearing the cloak and keeps the hood as it is.

## Not changed
- **Identified items traded back.** A report said items identified by another player came back unidentified. We could not reproduce this: identification carries over in both directions, between players and through the realm. If it happens to you, tell us whether the item showed its full name in the trade window before you accepted.
