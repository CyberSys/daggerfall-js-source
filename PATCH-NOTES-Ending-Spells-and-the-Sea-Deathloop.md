# Patch Notes: Ending your own spells, the sea deathloop and the character page's buttons

## New
- **End your own spells:** free the mouse (Free Mouse key, Y by default; offline, Enter too) and right-click a spell's icon on the HUD. On Enhanced Plus, the pause window's Stats tab also lists your spells under Effects, with an **End** button. Any helpful spell can be ended, whoever cast it (a potion's too); diseases, poisons, curses, harmful effects and the magic of items you wear or hold can't.
- **Enhanced Plus spell maker:** hold + or - to keep changing a value, faster the longer you hold, or click the number and type one.

## Fixes
- **Character page (F5, the pause window's Stats tab):** Spellbook, Pack and Chronicle open properly. No more spellbook stuck behind every screen, and Chronicle opens your notes and quests again. In a building they open the building's windows, and drops land on its floor. If a window can't open, the game resumes.
- **Sea deathloop:** online, dying at sea wakes you at the nearest temple, town or graveyard, even in another region. Autorun switches off when you come back, are resurrected or load a save. Your breath holds while a window is open, so you can empty your pack and swim up. "No More Puddles" never changed how weight drags a swimmer down: drop weight, or use Levitate, Water Walking or Water Breathing.
- **Shield Widget:** a shield repaired or damaged indoors looks the same outdoors, even if the widget was off in between.
- **Hotbar:** with the mouse freed, a click started in the world and released over the hotbar no longer leaves the button held.
- **Shop and trade lists:** tall item pictures stay inside their boxes.
- **Dungeon map:** loading a save made in a dungeon no longer greys its map or forgets other dungeons' maps, and the Burning Court no longer costs you one. Parts that vanish as you change floors are the floors above you, hidden (Return switches the view).
