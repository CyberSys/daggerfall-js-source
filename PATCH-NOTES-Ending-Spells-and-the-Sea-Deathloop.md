# Patch Notes: Ending your own spells, the sea deathloop and the character page's buttons

## Fixes
- **Spellbook stuck on screen (desktop app):** opening the spellbook from the character page (F5, the pause window's Stats tab) could leave it on screen behind every other screen with no way to close it. The page's Spellbook, Pack and Chronicle buttons now open their windows properly, the same way their keys do. The same fix covers the Pack button and F6 on that page.
- **Notes from the pause window:** the character page's Chronicle button opens your notes (and quests) again - it used to close the page and open nothing. Inside a building, the page's buttons open their windows in the building.
- **Stuck at the bottom of the sea:** online, dying in the open sea no longer brings you back on the seabed where you drowned. You wake at the nearest temple, town or graveyard, even when it's in another region. Autorun is switched off when you come back, so you don't run straight back into the water. Your breath also stops running out while the inventory is open underwater, so you can drop heavy loot and swim up.
- Note on "No More Puddles": that switch only changes the sea from the next time the world loads, and online it's always on for everyone. It never changed how heavy loot drags a swimmer down. Drop weight, or use Levitate, Water Walking or Water Breathing.
- **Shield looks broken outdoors after a repair:** a shield repaired indoors now looks repaired outdoors too, and a shield damaged indoors looks damaged outdoors.
- **Item pictures too big for their boxes:** in shop and player-trade lists, tall pictures (pauldrons, katanas) now stay inside their frames.
- **Dungeon map losing its colours:** loading a save made inside a dungeon no longer turns the whole explored map grey, and no longer forgets other dungeons' maps. If parts of the 3D map vanish as you go up or down a level and come back when you return, that's the map hiding the floors above you (switch the view mode with Return).

## New
- **End your own spells:** free the mouse (Enter, or your Free Mouse key) and right-click a spell's icon on the HUD to end it. Or open the pause window's Stats tab and pick Effects: every spell on you is listed with the rounds left, and an End button on the ones you can end. You can end your own helpful spells and helpful spells from party members. You can't end diseases, poisons, curses, harmful effects, or magic from equipped items.
- **Hold to repeat in the spell maker:** hold a + or - button and the value keeps changing, faster the longer you hold. You can also click the number between the buttons, type a value and press Enter. It's kept within that setting's range.
