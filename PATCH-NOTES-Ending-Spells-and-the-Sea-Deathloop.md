# Patch Notes: Ending your own spells, the sea deathloop and the character page's buttons

## Fixes
- **Spellbook stuck on screen (desktop app):** opening the spellbook from the character page (F5, the pause window's Stats tab) could leave it on screen behind every other screen with no way to close it. The page's Spellbook, Pack and Chronicle buttons now open their windows properly, the same way their keys do. The same fix covers the Pack button and F6 on that page. If a button's window can't open, the game resumes instead of leaving you with nothing on screen.
- **Notes from the pause window:** the character page's Chronicle button opens your notes (and quests) again - it used to close the page and open nothing. Inside a building, the page's buttons open their windows in the building, and pressing F5 in your pack opens the building's character page, not the street's. Dropping an item from that pack now lands on the building's floor.
- **Stuck at the bottom of the sea:** online, dying in the open sea no longer brings you back on the seabed where you drowned. You wake at the nearest temple, town or graveyard, even when it's in another region. Autorun is switched off when you come back, so you don't run straight back into the water. The same goes for a party member's Resurrect and for loading a save. Your breath also stops running out while the inventory is open underwater, so you can drop heavy loot and swim up. In dungeons it also waits while a prompt is up, such as Recall's.
- Note on "No More Puddles": that switch only changes the sea from the next time the world loads, and online it's always on for everyone. It never changed how heavy loot drags a swimmer down. Drop weight, or use Levitate, Water Walking or Water Breathing.
- **Shield looks broken outdoors after a repair:** a shield repaired indoors now looks repaired outdoors too, and a shield damaged indoors looks damaged outdoors. This also holds if the Shield Widget was switched off in between.
- **Mouse button stuck after the hotbar:** with the mouse freed, a click started in the world and released over the hotbar no longer leaves the button held (the view stuck, or the weapon swinging on).
- **Item pictures too big for their boxes:** in shop and player-trade lists, tall pictures (pauldrons, katanas) now stay inside their frames.
- **Dungeon map losing its colours:** loading a save made inside a dungeon no longer turns the whole explored map grey, and no longer forgets other dungeons' maps. If parts of the 3D map vanish as you go up or down a level and come back when you return, that's the map hiding the floors above you (switch the view mode with Return). Visiting the Burning Court no longer makes the game forget another dungeon's map.

## New
- **End your own spells:** free the mouse with your Free Mouse key (Y by default; offline, Enter works too, but online Enter opens the chat) and right-click a spell's icon on the HUD to end it. On the Enhanced Plus skin you can also open the pause window's Stats tab and pick Effects: every spell on you is listed with the rounds left, and an End button on the ones you can end. You can end any helpful spell on you, whoever cast it: your own, a party member's, a potion's. You can't end diseases, poisons, curses, harmful effects, or the constant magic of an item you hold or wear.
- **Hold to repeat in the spell maker:** hold a + or - button and the value keeps changing, faster the longer you hold. You can also click the number between the buttons, type a value and press Enter, Tab or click another button. It's kept within that setting's range.
