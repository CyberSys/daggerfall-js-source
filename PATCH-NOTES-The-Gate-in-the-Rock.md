# Patch Notes: Raiding Parties and the Gate in the Rock

## New: Raiding Parties
- **Towns across the Iliac Bay are raided** each game day by orcs, bandits or knights, online too. Help the town watch drive them off.
- **Defend a town** and the server records it: Renown for your character, the town's thanks in gold and a Magic-or-better piece, and sometimes a piece of the raiders' own set (Broken Oath, Thief-Taker's Garb, Orcsbane Harness).

## New: world events on the map
- **Towns under attack** show a red ring with crossed blades. Point at one to see the town, the attackers, how many have been driven off, and when they withdraw.
- **Point at an Oblivion Gate's ring** to see where it is, when it opens, seals or collapses, and who holds it.

## New: Oblivion Gates on Discord
- **Discord announces every gate** 15 minutes before it opens, with the times in your own clock. Take the World Events role to be pinged.
- **When the boss falls**, Discord says where, and who struck him down.

## Fixes
- **The gate in the rock:** an Oblivion Gate could rise inside World of Daggerfall's rocks, out of reach. The rocks now make way for it.
- **"The gate never left":** a sealed gate stands until midnight for anyone still fighting inside. The map, the banner and the chat now count down to its collapse.
- **Item cards** fit their window, and their buttons stay on screen.

## Also in this update
- **The dungeon map** is drawn by hand in 3D by default; the flat plan is still in Features.
- **Walk mode** can be bound to a key or a controller button.

## Notes
- The server restarts once with this update: reload the game (or restart the desktop app) to play online.
