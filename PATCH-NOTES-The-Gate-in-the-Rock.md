# Patch Notes: The Gate in the Rock

## Fixed
- **An Oblivion Gate could rise inside World of Daggerfall's rocks.** Its fire was out of reach, nobody could step in, and it stood until midnight. Rocks, camps and ruins now make way for the gate: nothing of the mod's stands within 24 m of it.
- **"The gate never left."** After it seals, a gate still stands until midnight (game time) for anyone fighting inside - that has not changed, but now it says so. The map, the gate's name and the banner by it count down to its collapse ("Sealed - collapses in 6:12"), a press on it says the same, and the chat's "has sealed" line says when it goes.

## New: world events on the map
- **Point at the gate's ring** on the map for its card: where it is, when it opens, seals or collapses, and who holds it (or that he fell).
- **Towns under attack** are marked on the map: a red ring with crossed blades. Point at one for the town, who is attacking, how many have been driven off, and when they withdraw.

## Also in this update
- **Raiding Parties**: towns across the Iliac Bay are raided each game day, online too. Help the town watch drive the raiders off.
- **The dungeon map** is drawn by hand in 3D by default; the flat plan is still in Features.
- **Item cards** fit their window, and their buttons stay on screen.

## Notes
- The server restarts once with this update: reload the game after it (or restart the desktop app) to play online.
