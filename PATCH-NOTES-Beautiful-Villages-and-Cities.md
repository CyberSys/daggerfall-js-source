# Patch Notes: Beautiful Villages and Beautiful Cities

## The towns of the Iliac Bay, laid out again
- **Beautiful Villages of Daggerfall** (by carademono) lays out every village, hamlet, farm, manor and temple in the Iliac Bay again, and rebuilds the roadside taverns.
- **Beautiful Cities of Daggerfall** (by carademono) lays out all 410 cities again, with districts, walls, gates, markets and docks.
- Both are on by default. Each has its own switch in the Mods pane. A change takes effect the next time the game loads, so a town never changes around you. Offline, the new towns also need Replace Game Artwork switched on.
- Every town is built from your own copy of Daggerfall, with the author's changes laid over it.

## Your house stays your house
- Your houses, rented rooms, quest buildings, items left with a smith, Recall anchors set indoors, and saves made inside a building all keep the town layout they were made in. If you switch either mod on or off later, that town keeps its old layout, so your furniture, chests and decorations stay where you left them.
- Once nothing of yours is left in a town, it follows the mods as they're set from the next load.
- A quest-giver you met indoors is still where you left them when you come back to finish the quest.
- Online, both mods are set for the whole room. Every online home keeps the layout its town had when it was bought, and everyone sees the same town around it.
- You can take any of a room's own furniture out of your house, including the new towns' food, tableware and clutter. Before this fix, some of those pieces couldn't be taken out online, and came back after a load offline. Some cabin pieces on Detailed Ships' ships had the same problem.

## Pieces from other mods, made for this game
- The two mods place pieces from other mods that this game doesn't include. This game draws its own versions instead of leaving gaps: coloured beds, framed paintings, tapestries and banners for the regions and the Eight Divines, beams, mantels and chimneys, food, animals and tools, rocks and hills, market stalls, docks with their ramps and steps, temple gardens, and crop fields that change with the climate and turn to stubble in winter.
- Detailed Ships' bottles and other items now also appear in the towns, even with Detailed Ships switched off.
- A few pieces are left out because they're effects, or because nothing is known of their shape, the same as in Daggerfall Unity without those mods. These include some bridges, chimney smoke, and the temples' deity statues.

## Fixes
- **Roleplay & Realism:** you can rest in the towns' coloured beds.
- **Roleplay & Realism:** the Master Armorer quest finds the armorer's shop in the new towns, instead of naming a neighbour's house.
- Windmills stand on the new farms when Windmills of Daggerfall is on, and the Order of the Raven's guild halls work in the new cities.
- You can enter the castles of Daggerfall, Sentinel and Wayrest in the new cities.
- The temples in two village layouts no longer have their front doors blocked by their foundations.
- A building no longer fails to load when it holds something the game has no picture for. Nothing is drawn there instead.
