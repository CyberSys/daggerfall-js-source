# Patch Notes: Marks

**Online only.** Marks open in stages. Developers get them first, then everyone. When your account has them, you'll see a **Marks** line on your account card.

## New currency: Marks
- **Marks** are the online world's own currency, kept by the account service. Nothing on your computer can make them, so they're worth the same for everyone.
- **Earn them by closing Oblivion Gates.** Each gate counted on your record strikes **50 Marks** to your account, up to two gates a day. Your chat says how many you got.
- **Sell them at any Bank of the Empire.** Open the Bank and use **Sell Marks**. The Bank pays **8 gold a Mark**, up to 300 Marks a day. The gold goes into your account at that bank, so it never weighs you down. You can't buy Marks with gold.
- **Guild treasuries hold Marks too.** On the Guild tab, any member can put Marks in. Only the guildmaster can take them out. A guild can't be disbanded while its treasury holds gold. Any Marks it holds go to the guildmaster when it disbands.
- Marks belong to your account, so all your characters share them. They can only be held on a registered account.
- Selling Marks at the Bank uses the Enhanced Plus UI.

Marks are what the coming professions, the Notice Board and the market will trade in.
