# Patch Notes: Oblivion Gates - the Warden's war songs

The boss music is louder and darker.

## Louder
- **The fight's music is much louder**: about 10 dB up in his first phase and 7 dB in the rest, well above the dungeon music it replaces.
- **It still never clips**, even at the highest music volume.
- Your music volume setting still controls it, and every other song in the game sounds exactly as before.

## Darker
- **No more bouncy plucked strings.** A low, driving brass figure pushes each phase instead.
- **A villain's harmony**: the fight moves between D minor and B-flat minor, with the bright major chords taken out.
- **New themes for the Warden** on low brass, built on grinding half steps where the old ones climbed to a fanfare.
- **Heavier drums**: low drums and toms in place of the hi-hat and tambourine.
- **A bell tolls low** at each section, and in his wrath it tolls the tritone.
- The music still grows with each phase, and **his fall still ends on its triumphant fanfare**, now louder too.
