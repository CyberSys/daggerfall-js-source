# Patch Notes: Town Map Marks

## Notice Boards on the town map
- Online, the town map now marks each **Notice Board** with a small pinned card, standing where the board is in the
  street.
- Point at one to see "Notice Board", and how many notes you haven't read yet ("Notice Board: 3 new").

## Player homes on the town map
- **Your home** is marked with a filled house.
- **Other players' homes** are marked with an outlined house. Point at one to see whose it is ("Gryphoth's home").
- A house you bought at a bank is marked too ("Your house"), in its own town.
- Homes appear a moment after the map opens if the town's homes are still loading.

## Where
- These marks are on the Enhanced town map (the default). The classic town map stays as it was in Daggerfall.
