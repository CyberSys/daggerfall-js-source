# Patch Notes: Sigil Stones

## Sigil Stones
- Sigil Stones stack. Stones already in your pack or wagon join into one stack the next time you load.
- Sigil Stones are bound: they cannot be traded to another player, dropped, sold, or put in a chest or a body. They go in your pack, your wagon or your own storage.
- A stone's card says it is bound.

## The Sigil Broker
- Prices are doubled: a set piece costs 4 stones (6 for a Legendary), a set weapon 6, a piece of Ruhn's Regalia 12.
- The Broker counts every stone in a stack. Locked stones are still never spent.
- Everything the Broker sells is bound: it cannot be traded, dropped or sold. It can still be repaired and identified. The Broker's card says so before you buy. Items bought before this update are not bound.
- You can dismantle anything the Broker sold you for half its price back in Sigil Stones, rounded down: 2 for a Rare piece, 3 for a Legendary piece or a weapon, 6 for a piece of Ruhn's Regalia. Enhanced Plus: open the item's card in your pack and press Dismantle. Classic and GrimoireUI: pick Remove and click the item (with no chest or wagon open), then answer Yes. Take the item off and unlock it first. Items bought before this update can't be dismantled.

## Classic and GrimoireUI
- Long messages in the inventory and shop windows wrap onto more lines instead of running off the screen.

## The Burning Court
- The way home that opens where the Warden falls no longer takes you out when you walk into it. Press it to leave, so you can pick up your spoils first.

No one is disconnected by this update.
