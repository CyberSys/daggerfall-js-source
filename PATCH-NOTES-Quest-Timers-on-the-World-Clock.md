# Patch Notes: Quest timers on the world's clock

## Online quests have their time limits again
- **Quest time limits are back online.** "You have 14 days", travel-time limits and the rest work as they do in
  Daggerfall again: a deadline can run out. This undoes the time-free quests from the last update.
- **A quest that only makes you wait moves on after a couple of minutes of play.** A letter, a meeting, a "come back in
  three days" arrives about two real minutes later online, and its text says "a few days". Only a quest's deadlines
  keep their days.
- **Quest timers run on the world's clock while you play.** A quest day is about two real hours of play.
- **Resting, waiting and travelling don't use up a quest's timers online.** An eight-hour rest, a long wait or a
  fast-travel journey takes nothing off a quest's clock. Only time you spend playing counts.
- **Time logged off never counts**, and neither does a tab left in the background.
- **Countdowns are back** for a quest's deadlines in the journal, the quest tracker, the quest rail and the map
  markers, along with the "running out of time" warnings. Quest text says a deadline's real number of days again.
- **Bounties lapse again** a day after you take them, on the world's clock: about two real hours, whether you rest,
  play or log off.
- **Quest letters wait for morning again**, as well as for you to be in town.
- **Vampire, werewolf, Thieves Guild and Dark Brotherhood quests** come after a few minutes of play online, one at a
  time (a cure can take ten or so), instead of after weeks.
- A failed quest online still costs its faction nothing.
- Offline play is unchanged: quest timers work as in Daggerfall, and a rest spends a quest's days.

## Gear
- Weapons and armour still last as long as in the last update (Daggerfall's own wear rate).
