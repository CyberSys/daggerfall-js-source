# Patch Notes: One bank account online

## Online
- **Your bank account is the same at every bank.** Online, the Bank of the Empire now keeps one account for you. Gold and letters of credit paid in at any bank can be drawn at any other. Before, each region kept its own account, so money deposited in one region was missing from every other region's bank.
- **Nothing to do on your side.** The next time you play an online character, the gold in all your regional accounts is moved into the one account, and the game tells you how much came in. No gold is lost or added.
- Houses, ships, guild deposits, the market, homes, decor, rent and the Marks all pay from and into the one account.
- A loan still belongs to the region where you took it. Its due date, its default and the reputation a default costs stay in that region. The account pays it back.
- **The Empire tells you when it takes your gold for a default.** If a loan goes into default, the Empire pays it from your account, and now says how much it took. Before, the gold left without a word.

## Offline
- Nothing changes. Each region keeps its own account, as in Daggerfall.
