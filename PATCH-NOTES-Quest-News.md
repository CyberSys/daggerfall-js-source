# Patch Notes: Quest news

## Quests (Enhanced interface)
- **Your journal tells you when it changes.** A notice slides in at the right edge when a quest begins, when its journal gains a new entry, when a deadline your journal gave you has less than a day left, and when it is completed or ends. Each one gives what happened and the quest's title, and a new entry or a deadline adds one line: the start of the entry, or the time left. A main quest's notice says "Main Quest".
- **One notice per quest.** If a quest changes twice in quick succession, you get one notice, not a stack of them - and it keeps the news that matters most: an ending over an entry, a deadline over an entry while it is still under a day.
- **Only real news.** An entry written again with the same words, or a clock the quest never told you about, is not news.
- **Read aloud.** A screen reader announces each notice once, whole.
- **It waits for you.** A notice's time only runs while the HUD is showing. News that arrives as you open a window is still there when you close it, and a deadline's notice then says the time left as it stands. Loading a game clears the notices of the game you left.
- **Your choice.** Turn it off under Features, Interface, "Quest news". With it off, the journal changes silently, as it does in Daggerfall.
- The quest's own messages and popups are unchanged, and the Classic interface is unchanged.
