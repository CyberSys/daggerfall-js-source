# Patch Notes: Fixes for Marks, Notice Boards, Bounties and Foraging

## Marks
- Selling Marks at a slow bank can no longer pay you twice.
- If a sale's reply is lost, your Marks are no longer lost when you sign out. The sale waits for your account and pays when it completes.
- One character's pending sale no longer blocks selling for your other characters.
- When a guild disbands, or its last member leaves, any Marks in its treasury go to the guildmaster. Before, leaving could destroy them.
- Closing an Oblivion Gate can no longer record the gate without paying its Marks.
- The bank's "counting" box gives up after 15 seconds instead of trapping you.
- "Sold today" updates as you sell.

## Notice Boards
- "Ask to join" and letter answers now open your letters. Before, the board closed and nothing opened.
- Brand-new or muted accounts can no longer hide notes by reporting them.
- A note hidden by reports stays visible to its author, who can take it down.
- A recruitment note stops recruiting if its author leaves the guild or can no longer invite.
- A note you are writing survives closing the board.
- Double-clicking Report or Take down no longer sends it twice.

## Bounties
- You can't take a bounty your party has already cleared today, and a clear only pays party members who took it first.
- Giving up a bounty and taking it again keeps your kills. It no longer summons a fresh pack.
- A party now gets one pack per hunt, whoever arrives first, and shares its kill count.
- Shared bounties pay rewards at your own level.
- A quickload inside a dungeon no longer doubles a bounty pack.
- A reward notice is never lost behind another window or a death.

## Death penalty
- You lose exactly what the death screen shows. A resurrection by a party member costs nothing.

## Foraging (online)
- Several tool uses in a row now wait their full time (they were capped at about a minute).
- Bonus finds and rewards held behind a wait survive a reload.
- Escape leaves a search again, and Enter works on buttons in the new windows.
