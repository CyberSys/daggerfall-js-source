# Patch Notes: Professions - Hunting, the Skinning Knife and Outfitting

## Hunting (online)
- A body **your own blow felled** can now be skinned where it lies, on the street or in a dungeon, until the day turns at midnight UTC. Bodies someone else killed, bodies inside buildings, and yesterday's bodies are searched as before.
- Ten foes give a hide: the **Rat** (Rat Pelt), **Giant Bat** (Bat Leather), **Grizzly Bear** (Bear Hide), **Sabretooth Tiger** (Tiger Pelt), **Spider** (Spider Silk), **Giant Scorpion** (Scorpion Chitin), **Slaughterfish** (Slaughterfish Scales), **Harpy** (Harpy Feathers), **Dreugh** (Dreugh Shell) and **Dragonling** (Dragonling Scale). Bigger game needs more Hunting: the bat and the bear 10, the tiger and the spider 25, the scorpion and the slaughterfish 40, the harpy and the dreugh 55, the dragonling 70.
- You need a **Skinning Knife** in your pack. With one, look at a body and the prompt says what **E** will do. Press the **act choice key** (the **Up arrow** by default; the prompt names it) to switch to **searching the body** instead: its loot is exactly what it always was. If you're standing right over the body, the prompt asks you to step back.
- **Hold E** with your crosshair on the first point of the dotted line across the body, and draw it along the line to the last point (mouse, right stick or a finger). Bigger game has more points: 5 to 9.
  - Follow the line closely, taking between 0.6 and 6 seconds from your first move along it, and it's a **clean pelt**: a one-in-two chance of a second hide.
  - Stray far from it and the pelt is **torn**.
  - Let go of E before the last point and the trace starts again. Press Escape to stop. Nothing is lost either way.
  - **Gentle acts**: just hold E for a little over a second.
- A skinned body gives its **hide** to your Stores. One body in four also gives Daggerfall's own ingredient: a **Big Tooth** (bear, tiger), **Spider's Venom**, a **Giant Scorpion Stinger** or **Dragon's Scales**. A torn pelt loses it.
- The rat, bat, bear, tiger, spider and scorpion also give a **Raw Meat**, and the slaughterfish a **Raw Fish**, to your Stores. The harpy, the dreugh and the dragonling give none.
- Hunting XP depends on the foe's tier, half as much again for a clean pelt. The XP message says if the pelt was clean or torn, or if a true line was drawn too quickly or too slowly to be clean.
- You can skin at any hour. You can't skin in a settlement, out at sea, with enemies nearby (in a dungeon too), or when fully encumbered. In a settlement or at sea, **E** opens the body's loot. Each body can be skinned once.
- Your **account** can take **30 hides a day** across all its characters, and only **3** of them from the harpy, the dreugh and the dragonling. A clean pelt's second hide counts, and the day's last skinning gives what's left. The Professions page shows how many you've taken today.

## The Skinning Knife (online)
- General Stores and Pawn Shops sell the **Skinning Knife** (100 gold, 0.5 kg, 50 uses) when you're playing online. You hold it like a dagger while you skin.
- It can also be made at the anvil from 1 Iron Ingot and 1 Pine Plank.
- Each skinning wears it by 1. When it breaks, you're told "Your Skinning Knife broke."

## Hunting's specialisations
- **Tracker** (50): outdoors, living foes you could skin within 100 m are marked on your compass.
- **Tanner** (50): hides cure one to one instead of two to one.
- **Butcher** (100): two Raw Meat a body instead of one, and the meat you withdraw spoils half as fast.
- **Trophy Hunter** (100) is listed but can't be chosen yet.

## The loom and the tanning rack
- A **Loom** section is on the Stores page, beside the Anvil and the Workbench. Use it inside a **Clothing Store** (50 gold a craft, cure or weave), or in your own home with a **Loom** station (DECOR, 50,000 gold).
- **Curing**: two hides make a leather. The rat, bat, bear and tiger make **Cured Leather**. The scorpion, slaughterfish, dreugh and dragonling make **Hardened Leather**.
- **Weaving**: three Spider Silk make a **Silk Bolt**. Harpy Feathers go into arrows (below).
- Curing and weaving give no XP; the skinning already did.
- At a Clothing Store, a recipe short of Linen or Wool offers **Buy from the Weavers** (Linen Bolt 2 Drakes, Wool Bolt 3 Drakes a bolt). If you don't hold enough Drakes, it says so.

## Outfitting
- **Leather armour**: cuirass, greaves, helm, pauldrons, gauntlets and boots at Daggerfall's Leather. Use **Cured Leather** from Outfitting 10 or **Hardened Leather** from 55. A cuirass takes 6, greaves 4, the rest 2. Hardened Leather makes a better piece.
- **Clothing**: every one of Daggerfall's 76 garments, men's and women's, in **Linen**, **Wool** or **Silk**. Small garments take one bolt, middle ones two, large ones three, and boots a bolt and a Cured Leather. Your own character's clothing is listed first.
- **Dye**: choose one of Daggerfall's **ten clothing colours** before you sew, or leave it undyed. Every garment takes a dye. The colour is sewn in: the piece keeps it wherever it goes, including on the market.
- **Rugs** (3 Wool Bolts), **tapestries** (4 Wool Bolts), **Large Skins** (2 pelts) and **Small Skins** (1 pelt), from a rat, bat, bear or tiger. These go among your things, to set down in your home.
- A **Fishing-Net** (2 Linen Bolts), Foraging's own.
- **The stitch**: the needle keeps a beat. Press **Space**, **Enter** or **Stitch** in time with it, eight times. All eight on the beat is a clean craft: one quality step up. A tap on **Stitch** counts the moment your finger goes down, and a key held down counts once. **Escape** sets the needle down (nothing is spent). **Quick craft** and **Gentle acts** work as they do at the anvil.
- Specialisations: a **Tailor** (50) sews clothing a quality step better and a **Leatherworker** (50) leather armour. **Couturier** and **Saddler** (100) are listed but can't be chosen yet.

## Arrows
- The workbench has a second arrow recipe: 1 Pine Plank, 1 Iron Ingot and 1 **Harpy Feathers** make twenty arrows, the same as the Twigs recipe.

## The Stores and the market
- **Every Stores material can be withdrawn to your pack now**, including Cured Leather, the Bear Hide, and Linen and Wool. The hides and leathers withdraw as their own items. The ingredients come out as Daggerfall's own, Raw Meat as Climates & Calories' Raw Meat, and Raw Fish as Climates & Calories' Raw Fish (Foraging's Fish if Climates & Calories is off).
- The market trades the new hides, leathers, and Linen, Wool and Silk Bolts. **Leather Armour**, **Clothing** and **Furnishings** can be listed and commissioned.

## Fixes
- The Professions page's Smithing unlocks were missing Silver, Elven and Dwarven, and Mithril. They're back.
- The anvil's **Strike** counts on your finger's press too, and a held key strikes once. Only one station's work runs at a time: with a forge and a loom at home, one Space no longer strikes and stitches at once.
- The Stores page keeps the field you're typing in, and the search keeps your cursor where you left it, when the page updates.
- In a dungeon, the Pick-Axe won't work with enemies nearby either.
- A harvest's second find (a Logging tree's Resin) now shows in the Stores straight away, not after the next refresh.
