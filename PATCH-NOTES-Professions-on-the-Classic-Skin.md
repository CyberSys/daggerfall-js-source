# Patch Notes: Professions on the classic skin

## Professions
- **New key: the Down arrow opens your Professions and Stores** on either UI skin. Before, these pages were only on the Enhanced pause menu, so classic-skin players online couldn't see their ranks or take gathered goods out of their Stores. You can rebind the key in Settings → Controls, under Professions.
- **Shop and home stations work on the classic skin.** The Forge, Workbench and Loom in shops, and the stations in your own home, now open the Stores page on either skin. Classic-skin players can also buy the Forge, Workbench and Loom for their homes.
- Your first harvest each session tells you which key opens your Stores.
- Offline, the key tells you that your professions are kept online.
