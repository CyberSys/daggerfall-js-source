# Patch Notes: Big saves online

Long-lived online characters can save again.

## The realm takes big saves
- **"This character's save is too big for the realm to take" is fixed for any normal save.** An online character's save had grown past the 4 MB the realm accepted in one piece, and every save after that was refused.
- **Online saves are now compressed** before they go to the realm, usually to a small fraction of their size. The realm takes saves up to 16 MB before compression.
- **Nothing to do on your side.** Press Play on the character. The realm still has the last save it took, and every save from then on lands as usual.
- Trades, guild deposits, houses and decor all work with the bigger saves too.
- An older copy of the game still reads the same character correctly. It just can't save one that has grown this big: update to keep playing it.
