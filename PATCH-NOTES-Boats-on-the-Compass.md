# Patch Notes: Boats on the compass

## Boats
- **Your boats are marked on the compass.** Every boat you've put in the water shows as a little teal boat on the compass, pointing the way to her from anywhere outdoors, however far off she is - in another town, across the bay, or in another region. If she's behind you, the mark waits at the end of the compass on the side to turn toward.
- **Find a boat you've lost track of.** A Large Boat's deed and a Rowboat's parts are used up when you put the boat in the water, so if you died aboard and woke at a temple, or simply forgot where you left her, follow the mark back to her.
- The boat you're steering isn't marked, and neither is one you're standing on or right beside.
- A boat you left in a dungeon's water points you to that dungeon.
- Works on both the Classic and Enhanced compass, and in the Overworld view.
