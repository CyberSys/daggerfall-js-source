# Patch Notes: Tired anglers, silent tools and falling foes

## Fishing
- **Fishing no longer walks you into a sudden death.** In a lake you fish while swimming, and treading water tires you as swimming does. If your fatigue runs out in the water you drown on the spot, whatever your health. Now, once you are down to the last quarter of your fatigue, the net won't cast in the water, and a cast already out ends. The prompt says **"too tired to fish in the water - get out and rest"**, with time left to swim to shore. On a pier or a boat's deck nothing changes: fish as long as you like.

## Gathering professions
- **Using a tool from your pack or hotbar now says it earns no profession XP.** The Wood-Axe, Pick-Axe, Sickle, Basket and Fishing-Net used from the pack are Foraging's own use: they fill your pack, not your Stores, and give no XP. They still do that, and now they say where the profession is done instead, for example: *"No Logging XP from this. Logging is done at a tree in the wilderness: walk up to one until the prompt shows, then press E."*
- **The Professions page explains how to gather** Herbalism, Mining and Logging, as it already did for Hunting and Fishing, and the empty Stores say where their goods come from.

## The bank
- **The bank window names the region whose account it shows** ("Account in Wayrest"), and lists the gold you've banked in other regions beneath it. Every bank online is the Bank of the Empire, but each region still keeps its own account: gold deposited in Daggerfall is in Daggerfall's account.
- Becoming a werewolf never touched your bank or your guilds, and still doesn't. (A vampire's guilds start over, as in Daggerfall, and come back with a cure.)

## Frame rate
- **Fixed the overworld frame rate sinking over time.** An enemy from a camp you had left behind could be restored by a load over ground that wasn't there yet, and fall forever, each second of the fall costing more. Enemies over ground that hasn't loaded now wait for it, then stand on it.

## Quests and houses
- **A townsperson who knows where a quest's own building is always marks it on your map** when you ask them outdoors (it used to be about one answer in four; the rest said "north of here").
- **A house a townsperson marked for a quest is ringed on the town map.** On the Enhanced town map it looked like any other house.
- **Houses' doors say "Residence"** and list **Go in** and **Buy it** when you point at them, and a click goes in. Clicking any house used to ask "This house can be your home ... Buy it?". The quest's house reads by its own name, "The <name> Residence", and is never for sale.

## The Overworld
- **No more light-blue rectangles on the sea in the Overworld view.** The surf along the coast was drawn from high above, where it showed as flat sheets over the water. It now shows only at ground level, as it should.
