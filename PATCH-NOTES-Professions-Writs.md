# Patch Notes: Professions - Guild Writs, the Guild Stores and Commissions

## Guild writs (online)
- A guild can post its own writs on the **Work** tab of a Notice Board, beside the Court's. A guild writ asks for a material from anyone's Stores and pays for it in Marks from the guild's **Marks treasury**.
- The **Guildmaster** can post guild writs. **Officers** can post them too, up to the **writ budget** the Guildmaster sets on the Guild tab: one amount that applies to every week until it's changed. The budget starts at 0, and it counts the Marks Officers put up in the week, withdrawn writs included. The week runs from Sunday 18:00 UTC.
- A writ asks for 1 to 5,000 units of any material the market trades. It pays a whole number of Marks per unit, up to half again the material's worth (so a tier-1 material pays at most 1 Mark). A guild can only post a writ its guild Stores have room for, counting what its other writs for that material still need.
- All of a writ's pay is held from the treasury when it is posted. A writ stands for **7 days** on the boards of the region it was posted in. A guild can have 20 writs up at once (writs that have run out don't count).
- **Anyone** can deliver to a guild writ, in the guild or not, except the guild's own **Officers and Guildmaster** (on any of their characters): they can take the guild Stores back out, so they could sell the guild the same units again and again. Deliver any number of units up to what it still needs and the guild Stores can still hold, from your Stores, at a board in its region. Units you bought go first.
- You're paid for what you deliver as you deliver it, less the market's **5% sales tax** (a guild writ is the guild's buy order). Guild writs give no Renown and no profession XP; those come from the Court's writs.
- The Guildmaster can withdraw any of the guild's writs, and an Officer can withdraw their own. Whatever pay is left goes back to the treasury. A writ that runs out returns what's left the next time anyone opens a Work tab.
- Every board's Work tab shows that region's guild writs under the guild's seal. **Yours** lists your guild's writs in every region.

## The guild Stores (online)
- Each guild now has its own **Guild Stores**, on the Guild tab. A guild writ's deliveries go there.
- **Any member** can put materials in from their own Stores, up to 5,000 at a time. **Any member** can take back what they put in of their own gathering. **Officers and the Guildmaster** can take out anything.
- What you gathered yourself and put in stays yours: take it back out and it's still your own harvest. Anything else that comes out of the guild Stores counts as bought. When you take materials out, your own deposit goes first, then the guild's, then other members' deposits.
- The guild Stores hold up to 50,000 of each material. The last 20 movements in and out are listed on the Guild tab. If a member leaves the guild, their deposit stays in the guild Stores, and it's theirs to take back if they rejoin.
- A guild can't be disbanded while its guild Stores hold anything, while one of its writs stands, or while a withdrawn writ's Marks are still waiting to go back to a full treasury. Empty them first.

## Commissions (online)
- **Commission a piece** on the Work tab: name a crafter by their username, choose the piece (a weapon, armour, a staff, a bow, a tool, a Repair Kit or furniture) and the lowest quality you'll take, and set the pay (1 to 1,000,000 Marks).
- The pay is held from your Marks for **7 days**. You can have 5 commissions up at once, and a crafter can have at most 20 waiting (ones that have run out don't count). Daedric and Warforged pieces can't be commissioned yet: nothing yields their metal.
- **Only the named crafter** can fill it, at a board in its region, with a piece they made themselves: unworn, at least the quality you asked, and not listed or set down in a home. The Work tab offers the crafter only the pieces that would fill it, lowest quality first. The piece goes from their pack to yours with its maker's record. You collect it on the **Market tab**.
- The crafter is paid your pay less the 5% sales tax. Arrows and siege works can't be commissioned.
- You can withdraw a commission while it stands, and the crafter can decline it (Decline asks you to press it twice). Either way, and if it runs out, your Marks come back.
- **A note can carry a "Commission a piece" button**: a crafter's advertisement. Press it and the Work tab opens the commission form with the author already named.

## Fixes
- Filling a Court writ for metal, stone or wood said you'd gained Herbalism XP. It now names the profession that actually gained it.
- The weekly Marks report (for the developers) now counts every Mark held in escrow: buy orders, auction bids, guild writs and commissions. Auction bids were missing.

## Fixes from the audit
- On the Notice Board's Work and Market tabs, a number you were typing kept losing focus, and the list jumped back to the top, whenever the board refreshed. Both now stay put.
- Every field on the Work tab's forms is labelled, and a button you can't press says why.
- Closing the board by accident no longer throws away a half-written writ or commission, and Escape closes an open form before the board.
- Listing, auctioning or filling a commission with a piece while not signed in no longer takes the piece out of your pack.
- A piece that's still being listed can't be handed to a commission at the same time, and the other way round.
- On the Guild tab, the guild Stores are read again each time you look, and a failed read offers Try again.

## Rollout
- These open wherever the professions and the Marks are, to the developers first. Everyone else's game is unchanged until they are switched on for all.
- Offline, nothing changes.
