# Patch Notes: Professions - Guild Writs, the Guild Stores and Commissions

## Guild writs (online)
- A guild can post its own writs on the **Work** tab of a Notice Board, beside the Court's. A guild writ asks for a material from anyone's Stores and pays for it in Marks from the guild's **Marks treasury**.
- The **Guildmaster** can post guild writs. **Officers** can post them too, up to the **writ budget** the Guildmaster sets each week on the Guild tab. The budget starts at 0, and it counts the Marks Officers put up in the week, withdrawn writs included. The week runs from Sunday 18:00 UTC.
- A writ asks for 1 to 5,000 units of any material the market trades. It pays a whole number of Marks per unit, up to half again the material's worth (so a common material pays at most 1 Mark).
- All of a writ's pay is held from the treasury when it is posted. A writ stands for **7 days** on the boards of the region it was posted in. A guild can have 20 writs up at once.
- **Anyone** can deliver to a guild writ, in the guild or not. Deliver any number of units up to what it still needs, from your Stores, at a board in its region. Units you bought go first.
- You're paid for what you deliver as you deliver it, less the market's **5% sales tax** (a guild writ is the guild's buy order). Guild writs give no Renown and no profession XP; those come from the Court's writs.
- The Guildmaster can withdraw any of the guild's writs, and an Officer can withdraw their own. Whatever pay is left goes back to the treasury. A writ that runs out returns what's left the next time anyone opens a Work tab.
- Every board's Work tab shows that region's guild writs under the guild's seal. **Yours** lists your guild's writs in every region.

## The guild Stores (online)
- Each guild now has its own **Guild Stores**, on the Guild tab. A guild writ's deliveries go there.
- **Any member** can put materials in from their own Stores. **Officers and the Guildmaster** can take them out into theirs.
- What you gathered yourself and put in stays yours: take it back out and it's still your own harvest. Anything else that comes out of the guild Stores counts as bought. When you take materials out, your own deposit goes first, then the guild's, then other members' deposits.
- The guild Stores hold up to 50,000 of each material. Every movement in and out is listed on the Guild tab.
- A guild can't be disbanded while its guild Stores hold anything or while one of its writs stands. Empty them first.

## Commissions (online)
- **Commission a piece** on the Work tab: name a crafter by their username, choose the piece (a weapon, armour, a staff, a bow, a tool, a Repair Kit or furniture) and the lowest quality you'll take, and set the pay (1 to 1,000,000 Marks).
- The pay is held from your Marks for **7 days**. You can have 5 commissions up at once, and a crafter can have at most 20 waiting.
- **Only the named crafter** can fill it, at a board in its region, with a piece they made themselves: unworn, at least the quality you asked, and not listed or set down in a home. The piece goes from their pack to yours with its maker's record. You collect it the next time you open the market.
- The crafter is paid your pay less the 5% sales tax. Arrows and siege works can't be commissioned.
- You can withdraw a commission while it stands, and the crafter can decline it. Either way, and if it runs out, your Marks come back.
- **A note can carry a "Commission a piece" button**: a crafter's advertisement. Press it and the Work tab opens the commission form with the author already named.

## Fixes
- Filling a Court writ for metal, stone or wood said you'd gained Herbalism XP. It now names the profession that actually gained it.
- The weekly Marks report now counts every Mark held in escrow: buy orders, auction bids, guild writs and commissions. Auction bids were missing.

## Rollout
- These open wherever the professions and the Marks are, to the developers first. Everyone else's game is unchanged until they are switched on for all.
- Offline, nothing changes.
