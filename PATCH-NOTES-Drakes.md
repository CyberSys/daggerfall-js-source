# Patch Notes: Drakes

## The currency has a new name
- **Marks are now called Drakes**, everywhere you see them: your account card, the Bank ("Sell Drakes", "8 gold a
  Drake"), the Market and Work tabs, writs, the professions' prices and your guild's **Drake treasury**.
- **Nothing else changed.** Your balance is exactly what it was, just under the new name.
