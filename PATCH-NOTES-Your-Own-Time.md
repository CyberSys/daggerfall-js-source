# Patch Notes: Your own time

**Your character now keeps their own clock online.** The world's clock is still shared: sun, moons, calendar, weather, shops and events. Nobody can move it.

## Two clocks
- Your clock runs while you play, and jumps ahead when you rest, wait, fast travel, train, serve a sentence, eat, drink, cook or hunt.
- It stops while you're logged off or dead. Time logged off is never charged to you.
- Time away is measured on the server's clock, not your computer's.
- On your clock: wounds, spells, hunger, thirst, sleep, diseases, poisons, vampirism and lycanthropy, skill checks, training, guild rank waits, guild letters, rented rooms, loans and repairs.
- On the world's clock: the sky, day and night, full moons, holidays, prices, shops, events, respawns and quest timers (played time only).

## Lycanthropy and vampirism
- An infection counts the days *you* live, rests and travel included. No more hours of play to turn.
- A vampire's thirst and a werewolf's urge run on your clock too, so a long rest makes you hungry.
- The sun is still the world's, so a bare-headed vampire can't fast travel by day. Raise a hood to go. The message now says how many real minutes until night falls (an hour at most).

## Rest and travel
- Resting online ages everything an hour of rest should: spells run out, diseases progress, hunger grows, skills get their check.
- Fast travel shows the journey's days again. They pass on your clock, and you arrive in the world's present. Cautious travel heals you again.
- Sailing from the Overworld tells you how many days of your time the voyage takes.
- In a party rest, each member's night passes on their own clock.
- Prison sentences are served on your clock, and you walk out rested.
- Collapsing from exhaustion costs a full hour and pays it back in full.

## Rooms, loans and repairs
- They read in your time: "7 days of your time (14h of play)". Resting uses it up; logging off doesn't.
- A loan that's already due says "now".

## Copying characters
- **Copy to offline** moves the world's timers onto your character's calendar. Quest time limits, journal dates, campfires and shop stock keep their distance, and the copy gets raids of its own.
- **Bring online** starts your character in the world's present. It is never charged for time away.

## Fixes
- Resting no longer rolls a disease's daily damage twice.
- Loading after an online rest no longer resets hunger and thirst for free.
- When your room runs out during a rest, the rest ends and the room is really gone.
- Conjured items run out during a long rest, as spells do.
- A town you saved from raiders stays saved when the world's clock is corrected, and pays you once. A guild shelf you bought out stays bought out.
- A character made online no longer gets a skill check the moment it's created.
- A save with a damaged clock loads safely instead of freezing the game.

Older saves load as before.
