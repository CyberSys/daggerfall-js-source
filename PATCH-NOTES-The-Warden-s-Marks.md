# Patch Notes: The Warden's Marks

## New: a different Warden every gate
- **Every Oblivion Gate, Valkynaz Ruhn comes marked:** one aspect and two trials, never the same as the gate before. Every combination comes round once in about nine days.
- **Four aspects:** the Burning (fire), the Rime-Wrought (frost), the Storm-Crowned (lightning) and the Venom-Blooded (poison). His fire becomes that element, and so does the ground it leaves. Your resistance to it counts: frost resistance helps against the Rime-Wrought.
- **Eight trials:** Colossal (larger and tougher, and his slam reaches further), Unyielding (his ward lasts longer and blows land lighter), Vengeful (he hits harder), Scarring (his slam and leap scar the floor), Grudge-Bearer (he hunts whoever hurts him most), Soul-Hungry (each fallen challenger heals him), Dagon's Favoured (his fire comes a phase early) and Echoing (every meteor falls twice).

## More detail in the fight
- **Tonight's marks are announced** with the gate's omen, on the map when you point at its ring, and on Discord.
- **As you step through** the Warden's marks are named. His bar shows tonight's epithet and trials, and names every attack as it comes tonight.
- **Each aspect has its own look and sound:** icy, stormy or venomous telegraphs, glow and ground, with the element's own sounds.

## Fixes
- **The Warden can no longer be pacified.** A Pacify or Charm aimed at him is refused, and he says so.

## Notes
- The server restarts once with this update: reload the game (or restart the desktop app) to enter a gate.
