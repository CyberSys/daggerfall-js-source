# Patch Notes: Notifications Fixed

## Enhanced Plus notifications
- **Notifications look like Enhanced Plus again.** Since the Notice Board arrived, toasts and message boxes had a brown
  corkboard patch behind their words, and a message box's "click or press a key" line turned brown italic. Both are
  gone.

## Notice Board
- The small helper lines on the Notice Board's forms (pinning a note, posting a notice) are the board's own again:
  italic and left-aligned, no longer in the notifications' capitals with a rule above.
- The board's cork can now fill the window's full height before it scrolls.
