# Patch Notes: Professions - Herbalism, the Stores and Court Writs

## Herbalism (online)
- Online, **herb patches** now grow in the wilderness: small clusters of the plant itself, on open ground away from towns, water and cliffs. Every player sees the same patches, and they change each day.
- Walk up to a patch and the prompt at the bottom of the screen tells you what it is and what **E** will do.
- A **common herb** comes up by hand in under a second.
- An **uncommon or rare herb** needs a **Sickle** (Foraging's) and enough Herbalism: uncommon at 10, rare at 25. Rare herbs grow only where players have confirmed the land. Hold **E** for two and a half seconds and keep your hand steady. Turn your view more than a few degrees, or step away, and the herb is **bruised**: one fewer comes up. If it is not bruised, it is a **clean** pick, worth half as much XP again. Let go of E early, or press Escape, and nothing is lost.
- A patch also holds food. Press **;** at a patch to switch to searching with a **Basket**: three glints show one after another, and attacking while each one shows finds it. The more you find, the more you gather.
- Each patch gives its herbs once and its food once a day. You can gather **60** times a day, and only in daylight (7:00 to 17:59).
- The herbs follow the seasons: winter bares the flowers, roses, poppies and berries. Spring's blooms and autumn's berries give half as much again.
- A completed act wears its tool by 1, as Foraging's own uses do.

## The Stores
- What you gather goes to your character's **Stores**, not your pack. The Stores hold up to 5,000 of each material.
- The **Stores page** (on the Enhanced pause menu's Stats rail) lists everything, with filters, a search and sorting. **Withdraw to pack** takes up to 200 at a time.
- A withdrawn herb is Daggerfall's own ingredient, **northern or southern** by the region you picked it in. It works in the potion maker like any other. Nothing goes back into the Stores from the pack.

## Professions
- The **Professions page** (also on the Enhanced pause menu's Stats rail - the Classic interface's pause menu has no pages yet) shows all thirteen professions. Herbalism is the first you can train: ranks 0 to 100, from Novice to Master, with XP to the next rank and today's harvests.
- At ranks **50 and 100** you choose a **specialisation**. The first choice is free. Changing it later costs 1,000 Marks and takes a week, and the old one keeps working until then.
- **Gentle acts** (a checkbox on the Professions page): every act completes plainly - no bruising, and no clean bonus.
- A toast says what each harvest brought, and a banner marks ranks 25, 50, 75 and 100.

## Court Writs
- The Notice Board has a new **Work** tab: the region's **Court writs**, under the Court's seal. Each asks for a number of one herb and pays **Marks** and **Renown**.
- **Take** fills a writ from your Stores. A writ is filled whole, by the first player to take it. You can fill **3** a day.
- A region's Court posts writs once a player registered for a week has gathered there. The more players are active on the server, the more writs are posted.

## Foraging
- Online, the shops stock Foraging's six tools even with the Foraging mod switched off, because the professions need them. The foods still follow the switch.

## Rollout
- The professions open to the developers first. Everyone else's game is unchanged until they are switched on for all.
- Offline, nothing changes.
