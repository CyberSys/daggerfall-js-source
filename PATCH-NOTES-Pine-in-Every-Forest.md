# Patch Notes: Pine in every forest

## Professions
- **New loggers can chop in every forest.** Pine, the tree a Novice can fell, used to grow only in the mountains. The Woodlands, Haunted Woodlands and Swamps had only Oak, which needs Logging 10, and Rainforests and Subtropical lands usually had no tree you could fell at all. Now about 2 trees in 5 in those forests are Pine, so you can start Logging wherever you live.
- The rest of each forest is unchanged: Oak and Cherry in the Woodlands, Teak and Mahogany in the Rainforest, and the rare Ironwood and Ghostwood as before.
- Court writs can now ask for Pine Logs in those regions.
