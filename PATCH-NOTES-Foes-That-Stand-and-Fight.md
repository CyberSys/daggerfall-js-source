# Patch Notes: Foes That Stand and Fight

Feedback on Smarter Foes: foes kept walking backwards, archers kited you in circles, and fights felt too hard. These changes only apply with **Enhanced AI** on.

## No more walking backwards
- **Walk up to a foe and it fights you.** A foe waiting for its turn used to back away as you came closer, so you could chase it around the room. Now it turns and fights.
- **The step back after a blow is short.** A foe that just hit you steps back for a moment (under half a second) and then holds its ground. Chase it and it fights back.
- **Retreating foes walk away facing the way they're going.** A hurt foe backing off, or an archer making room, turns around and walks off, then turns back to fight. No more moonwalking.
- **Hurt foes back off less often.** At most once every 10 seconds.

## Archers
- **No more circling.** An archer waiting for its turn to shoot stands still and faces you. It doesn't strafe around you.
- **One retreat, then it fights.** An archer you close in on walks back once, for up to 2 seconds. If you catch it, or it backs into a wall, it fights you hand to hand. It won't retreat again for 8 seconds.
- Once you're more than about 6 m away again, it goes back to shooting.
