# Patch Notes: Palace terms, lost boats and field fixes

## Phones
- **You can accept a palace guard's terms of entry on a phone.** The row of touch buttons (with **abc** for the keyboard and **⏎** for Return) now shows for every window inside castles, dungeons and buildings, not only in the street. Tap **abc**, type your answer, then **⏎**.

## Boats
- **Boats lost before the sea update's fixes are given back.** A Large Boat or Rowboat stood under the ground or on the seabed (you could hear it, but never find it) is packed into its parts in your inventory the next time you come near it. Use the parts to place it again on the water. A crewed ship is left where it is: use its deed at a port to call it.

## Temples
- **Temple libraries open at the rank the temple promises.** A temple's bookshelves refused every member at every rank. They now let you read from the library rank your deity grants (Curate, rank 4, for Stendarr).

## The Overworld
- **Spawned dungeons that have gone are off the map.** An Elite (or any spawned) dungeon whose time had run out kept its marker, and its walk, whenever you came near it again. It no longer does.

## Bounties
- **A dungeon bounty's pack stands where you can reach it.** The lair is now one of the dungeon's own quest spots - where Daggerfall puts every quest's target - instead of any monster's place, which could be a sealed room or under water.

## Online
- **"World: Sign in to play online" no longer repeats for ever on a slow connection.** The game waits longer for the sign-in to answer before it joins. If it still fails, the game's console now says why.

## Crashes
- **Fixed a crash when the game was minimised or put in the background while a world was loading** ("Cannot access 'be' before initialization").
