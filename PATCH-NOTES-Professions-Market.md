# Patch Notes: Professions - The Market

## The Market (online)
- Every **Notice Board** has a **Market** tab beside Notices and Work. Stand at any board in a town to use it.
- Everything on the market is priced in **Marks**.
- There are five views: **Materials**, **Crafted**, **My listings**, **Orders** and **History**.

## Selling
- List anything in your **Stores** (1 to 5,000 units), at a price of 1 to 1,000,000 Marks each, the whole listing worth no more than 10,000,000 Marks. The units leave your Stores at once. Units you bought go first, so your own stay for Court writs.
- List a **crafted piece** from your pack, or crafted furniture from your home that isn't set down, at one price for the piece. It leaves your pack while it is listed. Only its owner can list it, and only once at a time. Its wear goes with it. Pieces you are wearing, locked or bound pieces, pieces enchanted since they were made, arrows and pieces still on their way to you can't be listed.
- A listing stands for **72 hours** on every board of the **region** you listed it in. You can have 30 up at once.
- Listing costs a fee of 1% of the listing's worth, at least 1 Mark. The fee isn't returned if you cancel.
- When something sells, you are paid at once, wherever you are: the price less a **5% sales tax**. The tax is reckoned on everything the listing has sold, so a listing bought in parts pays the same as one bought whole.
- Cancel a listing whenever you like: a piece comes back to your pack, and materials to your Stores - if your Stores can't take them back, the cancel is refused until there's room. Goods that don't sell within 72 hours come back the next time you open the market (materials once your Stores have room).

## Buying
- Buy materials in part or whole, and crafted pieces whole.
- Something listed in **your board's region** is yours at once: materials go into your Stores, and a piece goes into your pack (or your home, for furniture).
- Something listed in **another region** comes by **courier**. You pay a courier fee on top, by the load and the distance between the two regions' main towns, and it arrives after the courier's time: 15 minutes, plus a minute for every 10 map pixels. **On the road**, at the top of the tab, shows what is on its way. Materials go into your Stores when they arrive (if your Stores are full, they wait until there's room). Pieces are handed to you when you open the market, or as they arrive while it is open.
- A signature material is cheap at home and dear abroad, so hauling is a trade.

## Buy orders
- Post a standing order for any material (up to 5,000 units, at your price each, no more than 10,000,000 Marks in all) at a board. The Marks are held when you post it. Materials nothing yields yet - Daedric and Warforged ingots, Bear Hide - can't be ordered.
- Anyone at a board in that region can **fill it from their Stores**, in whole or in part. They are paid the price less 5% (reckoned on everything the order has bought), and the units go into your Stores at once.
- An order stands for **7 days**. Withdraw it whenever you like, and whatever wasn't filled comes back. You can have 20 up at once.

## History
- Every material traded this week, on the market or through a buy order, shows its **7-day median** price and a small line of the week's days. The Materials view shows them too, and the Orders view the median.
- **Your trades** lists your last 20 sales and purchases, and the buy orders you filled or had filled.

## The Weavers' counter
- On the Materials view: **Linen Bolt** 2 Marks and **Wool Bolt** 3 Marks a bolt, straight into your Stores. Bolts stay there until their craft is practised.

## Moderation
- Report a listing and a moderator will look at it. A moderator can remove a listing, and its goods go back to their seller.

## Fixes
- A craft whose answer was lost waited to be settled until you also withdrew something. It now settles on its own.
- Buying from the smith's or the furnisher's stock now updates the Marks shown at the Bank.
- The workbench's plane now draws its board properly: the grain as a line and your stroke visible (it drew as a black shape before).

## Rollout
- The market opens to the developers first. Everyone else's game is unchanged until it is switched on for all.
- Offline, nothing changes. A piece bought on the market is an ordinary item in your pack anywhere.
