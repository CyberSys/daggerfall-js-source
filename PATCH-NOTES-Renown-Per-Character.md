# Patch Notes: Renown Per Character

## Renown
- **Each character has its own Renown again.** Every character keeps its own level, and the level beside a name is that
  character's.
- **Nobody loses a level.** Each character starts from its own Renown, plus everything your account earned while
  Renown was shared.
- **A character made while Renown was shared** starts with what the account earned in that time.
- **Renown still comes at three quarters** of the old rate, and the hourly limit is still shared by all your characters.
- **A town defended pays the character that fought for it**, at that character's own level.
- **A guild is founded on the founding character's own Renown.** It needs Renown 10.
- **A Court writ's Renown goes to the character who delivers it.**
- **The account card shows a row for each character's Renown.** The level beside your name is the character you
  played last.
- **Deleting an online character deletes its Renown too.** The delete window says so again.

## Behind the scenes
- The account service updates. Nobody is disconnected.
