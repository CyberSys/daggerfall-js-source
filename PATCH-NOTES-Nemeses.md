# Patch Notes: Nemeses

## Nemeses
- **Elite and Champion foes (level 3+) that kill you become your nemesis.** They get a name of their own and an epithet for what they did, e.g. "Grushnak the Butcher" or "Varis, Bane of Ayla".
- **They can also run.** Below 20% health, a special foe has a small chance (5%) to break and flee. If it gets away, it becomes a nemesis too ("the Scarred", "Who Ran"). Kill it before it escapes and nothing comes of it.
- **They come back.** One to three in-game days later, a nemesis can turn up in place of a wilderness encounter. Only one comes at a time, the strongest first.
- **Stronger every time.** Each time a nemesis kills you or escapes, it ranks up (up to rank 5) and earns a new epithet. Each rank adds health (+25%) and damage (+10%). Human foes also gain levels.
- **They taunt you, face to face.** When a nemesis spots you, a card slides in at the left of the screen (styled like the notifications) with its portrait, name, rank and title, and its words typed out in its own voice: what it did, and your name. Beasts snarl instead. Cards also mark the moments that matter: a foe breaking to flee ("This isn't over!"), an escape, its last words when you finally kill it, and its gloat when you come back after it killed you.
- **A Nemeses page.** The pause menu's Stats rail has a new Nemeses page: every nemesis with its portrait, rank, title, what it has done to you, and when it will come for you next (Hunting, Biding, or Abroad), plus the Fallen ones you've put down.
- **Spells and poison count.** A foe whose spell, lingering effect or poisoned blade finishes you becomes your nemesis just like one that cuts you down.
- **Dungeons too.** Special foes can flee and get away inside dungeons as well (offline, or any foe that's yours alone when online).
- **Everyone sees the name.** In online play, other players see your nemesis by its name.
- **Better loot.** A slain nemesis drops extra gold and has a chance at Magic, Rare and Legendary gear, rising with its rank. From rank 3 it always drops a Rare.
- **Slain for good.** Kill a nemesis and it's gone: "Grushnak the Butcher has fallen. Your nemesis is no more."
- **They remember even if you reload.** Nemeses are tied to your character and survive an offline death or loading an older save. Up to 5 can be alive at once.
- Nemeses follow the **Loot rarity** setting. Turn it off and none are made.
- The taunt card is part of the Enhanced UI. On the Classic skin the same lines appear as text.
- The card is a HUD piece like any other: unlock the UI (Alt+U) to move it.

## Elites
- **Elites are rarer.** In the open world, 2% of foes (was 5%), and only one at a time: none while another elite is nearby, and none for about 15 minutes of play (3 in-game hours) after the last. A camp or a pack never brings more than one. Ordinary dungeons hold one 10% of the time (was 20%). Elite Dungeons keep their 3 to 4.
- **Elites no longer appear below foe level 3,** and never as city watch or allies (same rules as Champions).
- **Elites are named everywhere:** on hover, in death notices and on their corpses, not just the target bar. Champions, elites and nemeses are now named on hover even while hostile.
