# Patch Notes: Repairs, Prices, Party Maps, Dress and the Journal

## Repairs
- **Repairs cost a third less.** Every smith and guild now charges two thirds of the old repair price, online and offline.
- **Instant repair is on by default.** Smiths hand your gear back straight away instead of keeping it for days. You can still turn it off in the settings.
- **New: Field Repair Kits.** Find them in dungeon treasure piles and on enemies that carry loot. A Field Repair Kit mends any weapon or armour, whatever its metal, by 15% of its condition (the most worn piece first), then it's used up. Smith-made Repair Kits still mend 25% of their own metal.

## Cheaper essentials online
Online, these now cost half:
- Tavern rooms, food and drink
- Temple disease cures
- Travel fares (inn nights and ship passage)
- Potions, at every shop and guild

Other items, repairs, training and spells keep their prices. Offline prices are unchanged.

## Spell absorption toned down
- Items with **Spell Absorption** now absorb Destruction spells 50% of the time instead of always.
- **Spell Absorption** spells max out at a 50% chance. Custom Spell Absorption spells can't go above 50.
- Absorbing a spell now restores **half** its cost in magicka instead of all of it.
- Sorcerers (and other classes that always absorb) still absorb every Destruction spell they have room for, with the half refund.

## New spell: Shared Cartography
- A **Mysticism** spell. While it lasts, the parts of a dungeon you map appear on the automaps of party members in the same dungeon. A scout can map ahead for everyone.
- **How to get it:** buy it ready-made at the spell shop while playing online (listed next to Resurrection), or make your own in the Spell Maker (cast on self, duration only).
- The ready-made spell lasts about 2 minutes at level 1 and a bit over 3 at level 10. Recasting adds to the time left, so keeping it up is part of the delve.
- Areas you receive from party members appear greyed out: known, but not visited by you.
- Offline or without a party, the spell does nothing.

## Dress for the part
What you wear now changes how people react to you:
- **Common clothes** (casual pants, peasant blouses, plain robes and the like) win over commoners but draw disdain from nobles.
- **Formal wear** (gowns, formal cloaks and tunics, surcoats) impresses nobles and pleases merchants, though commoners find it a little stuck-up.
- **Priest and Priestess Robes** earn a warmer welcome at the temples and a small nod from scholars.
- Each garment counts a little, capped at ±10 per group, and only while you wear it.
- The Enhanced Plus **Standing** page has a new **Dress** line showing what your outfit is doing.

## A tidier journal (Enhanced Plus)
- Finished quests have a **Remove** button, and **Clear archive** removes them all at once (each asks you to click twice).
- Active quests can be **hidden** from the journal without abandoning them. They keep running, and their timers and HUD markers stay.
- **Show hidden** brings hidden quests back into view, each with an **Unhide** button. Your hidden list is kept in your save.
