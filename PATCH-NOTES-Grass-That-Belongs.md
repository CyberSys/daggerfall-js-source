# Patch Notes: Grass that belongs

## The grass
- **Grass is no longer dark.** Up close, tufts of grass used to look almost black against the ground they grew from, and darker still at dusk, in rain and in storms. Grass is now painted in the ground's own colours: the base of each tuft matches the ground under it, and the blades get lighter towards their sunlit tips.
- **Grass blends into the land.** Grass takes its colour from whatever ground it grows on, so it matches in every climate and season: the lush green of the woodlands, the grey-green of the mountains, the deep green of the swamps.
- **No more yellow band.** The grass a little way off used to show as a yellowish stripe across the field. Now the far grass fades into the ground's own colour.
- **Grass is lit like the ground.** At dawn, at dusk, under clouds and by moonlight, the grass now takes exactly the same light as the ground under it, with Enhanced lighting on or off.
- **Shade falls on the grass.** Grass in the shade of a tree, a wall or a passing cloud is now shaded like the ground around it, instead of staying in full sun.
- **Rain doesn't darken the grass twice.** Grass used to be darkened for bad weather on top of the dimmer light the weather already brings. Now it's darkened once, like everything else.
- **Lanterns and torches light the grass.** At night, grass near a lantern, a campfire or the torch in your hand now glows along with the ground under it, instead of staying dark beside the lit ground.
- **Grass on hillsides.** Grass on a slope facing away from the sun is now shaded like the slope, instead of staying as bright as grass on level ground.
- **Texture mods.** With a texture mod that replaces the ground, grass now takes its colours from the mod's ground.
- **Classic lighting.** With Enhanced lighting off, grass is no longer brighter and greener than it is with Enhanced lighting on.

The grass is under *Sight* in Features: **Grass** sets how much grass grows and its style (Pixel or Smooth). Both styles get the new colours and light.
