# Patch Notes: Gear Lasts Longer, Potions Are Easier to Find

## Weapons and armour wear like Daggerfall again
- **Gear wears at Daggerfall's own rate.** Two of the combat mods made weapons wear several times faster than the original game, and armour many times faster. Their wear options are now off by default, online and offline.
- **Claws and bites no longer wear your armour.** Only blows from weapons do, as in the original game.
- **Broken enchanted gear is no longer destroyed.** It breaks and stays in your pack, and a smith can repair it.
- **Upkeep costs far less.** Gear wears much more slowly, and repairs cost half what they did (below).

## Repairs
- **Repairs cost half as much.** Every smith and guild now charges a third of Daggerfall's repair price, down from two thirds. With the slower wear, keeping a Daedric longsword in shape costs about a seventh of what it did, and Daedric armour far less.
- **Repair kits mend up to 75%.** Field Repair Kits and smith-made Repair Kits now restore a piece to three quarters of its condition at most. That keeps your gear working in the field; a smith restores it fully, and a freshly sharpened blade hits harder than a worn one.
- If everything you could mend is already at 75% or more, the kit tells you so and isn't used up.

## Healing potions
- **Alchemists always stock Potions of Healing.** Every alchemist has a few each day (more in a better shop), and general stores keep one or two. Online they're half price, like every potion.
- **You'll find more of them.** Enemies that carry loot sometimes have one, and dungeon treasure piles hold one more often than before.

## Companions' packs
- **Your crew companions carry what a person of their strength can.** A companion's pack now has a weight limit based on their strength, about 90 kg for a typical hand. The pack window shows how much they're carrying.
- When a pack is full, the companion says they can't carry any more. If you store gold, they take only what they can carry.
- Packs that were already over the limit keep everything. You can always take things out; you just can't add more until there's room.

## Good to know
- Offline, you can turn the old wear back on in the Mods menu, under Physical Combat And Armor Overhaul and Roleplay & Realism. If you had changed these options before, they've been reset once.
- Online, everyone plays with them off.
