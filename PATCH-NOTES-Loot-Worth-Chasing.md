# Patch Notes: Loot worth chasing

Everything below is part of **Loot rarity**, which is on by default. With it off, loot is exactly Daggerfall's.

## Better rolls
- **Skill bonuses fit the item.** A sword's skill bonus is usually its own blade skill or a fighting skill like Critical Strike; armour leans to the body's skills; rings and amulets to magic, talk and stealth. Languages still turn up, just rarely.
- **See how good a roll is.** Every rolled line on a Magic or Rare card (and an Exalted Legendary's extra line) now shows its range, like `+22% damage [10-25]`. A Rare with every line at the top of its range is a **Perfect Rare**.
- **Exalted Legendaries.** One Legendary in ten drops **Exalted**: it carries one extra line, rolled from the top half of the Legendary range, and is worth far more.

## Thirty Legendaries, each with a power
- **Twenty new Legendaries**, thirty in all, each with its own lore.
- **Every Legendary has a power** that does something - Nightwhisper's *Silent Death* doubles a blow on a foe that hasn't noticed you; others chain lightning between foes, siphon souls, hold you up at the brink in a *Last Stand*, or bite deeper into dragons. A power shows on the card and, when it has a count or a timer, as a chip on the HUD. You get each power once, however many pieces carry it.
- **The Last Lock**, the Dwarven Thunderlock's own Legendary, has a power too: *Dwemer Defiance*, +50% damage against daedra and atronachs.
- **Affixes that do things.** Magic and Rare weapons, armour and jewellery can now roll a line that acts: extra fire, frost or shock damage on a hit, life leech, thorns that hurt a foe whose blow lands, cheaper spells, or extra damage against the undead, daedra, humanoids or animals.

## The chase
- **Signature drops.** Each Legendary is found among one kind of foe - the undead, the daedra, dragons, beasts, orcs and giants, casters, rogues or warriors - and drops from them five times as often as any other. The kind of dungeon matters too: a crypt leans to the undead's own.
- **Champion foes.** About one foe in twenty (level 3 and up) is a **Champion**, named by its trait - *Mighty*, *Stalwart*, *Swift*, *Vampiric* or *Thorned*. Twice the health, hits a quarter harder, and always carries a Rare or better. Online, everyone sees the same champion.
- **Bad luck runs out.** Every piece you loot below Legendary makes the next Legendary likelier - a little after 25 pieces, up to three times as likely after 250. Looting a Legendary resets it. A piece only counts the first time it's ever picked up.

## The loop
- **Welkynd Shards.** Break a Magic piece or better that you found into shards: Magic 1, Rare 3, Legendary 8, Exalted 15. Use **Salvage** on the item's card in your pack, or at the Mages Guild. Crafted pieces don't break. Shards are bound to you and stack.
- **The Reforge.** Talk to the Mages Guild's Identify service and choose **Reforge** (press **F** in the classic window). Pick a line on a Magic or Rare piece - or an Exalted Legendary's extra line - and roll it again: 2 shards and 100 gold (Magic), 4 and 400 (Rare), 10 and 2,000 (Exalted). Once you reforge a line, only that line can be rerolled on that piece. A Rare keeps both halves of its name.
- **The Codex.** Every Legendary and Aetheric piece you find is recorded, with the day you found it, and the first find is announced with a fanfare. Open it from the **Codex** button in your pack (Enhanced UI) or the Reforge's Codex tab: unfound Legendaries show where they're said to be found.
- **Imprint.** At the Reforge, a Rare can take the power of a Legendary of the same kind (weapon, armour or jewellery) that's in your Codex, for 20 shards and 5,000 gold - if the power suits it: *Chain Lightning* only goes on a bow or the Thunderlock, *Earthshaker* only on a melee weapon. Once per piece; it stays Rare.
- **Crafted pieces on the market.** A crafted piece you've reforged or imprinted can't be listed on the market, since the market would remake it without the change.
- **A line of light over every find.** A body or pile holding a Rare or better shows a thin line of light in its best tier's colour - the nearest eight within 40 m, in dungeons, towns and buildings. Legendaries pulse.
