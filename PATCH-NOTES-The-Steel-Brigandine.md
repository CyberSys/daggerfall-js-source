# Patch Notes: The Steel Brigandine

## Morrowind models
- **The Steel Brigandine has its own look.** With Roleplay & Realism: Items on, a Brigandine Jerkin made of steel is now drawn as a red, riveted brigandine with a belt, front clasps and a skirt, instead of Morrowind's plain steel cuirass. It is the second model made for this port alone, after the Dwarven Thunderlock.
- It shows on your character in third person, on other players, and on the paper doll. As with any cuirass, first person shows only your arms.
- It moves with your body as you move, and the skirt bends with your legs.
- **It sits on your torso.** It used to be drawn too low on the Morrowind body, and because it takes the chest's place, your character's chest and shoulders were missing above it. It is now fitted to your own body's chest when it is put on, its collar at the base of your neck, whatever your race or sex.
- Brigandine Jerkins of every other material, and the classic Steel Cuirass, look as they did before.
- If you keep your own Morrowind files, a mesh or texture of the same name in your Data Files still replaces this one, as it does for any Morrowind model.

## Known issues
- On the ground and in your pack's icons, the jerkin still shows the steel cuirass.
