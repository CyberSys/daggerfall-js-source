# Patch Notes: Out of the pit, and quieter at the helm

## Climbing
- **Tall dungeon walls can be climbed again.** Dungeon walls are built from stacked sections. Where the section above sticks out a little, such as the edge of a doorway or a pillar, your head used to hit it about 1.4 m up and the climb stopped. That's how a "two blocks" wall left you stuck in a pit. Now you shift along the wall past it and keep climbing.
- **Low Climbing skill gets you off the ground.** Some large dungeon walls stopped a climber with low Climbing skill right at the floor. You now climb them at your normal pace.
- **No more falling off at gaps in a wall.** Some dungeon walls have a thin gap between two sections. The climb used to treat the gap as a ledge, grab it, then let go and drop you to the floor, again and again. It now climbs straight past it.
- **Climbing past a step in a wall.** Where the upper part of a wall sits a little further back than the lower part, you used to stop at the step. Now you reach for the wall above and carry on to the top.
- Checked across 25 dungeons, including Ruins of Old Carololda's Farm: every wall the old climbing could get you over, the new climbing gets you over too. The exceptions are slopes steeper than 50° and a ledge with something standing right behind it. You can't climb onto those, but you can always get down: press **Jump** to push off, **Back** to climb down, or **Crouch** to let go.

## Travel
- **Enemies no longer get free hits during a hunt.** When you're offered a hunt on the road ("You spot a set of animal tracks..."), an enemy that comes near now closes the offer, or ends the search if you've already started, so you can turn and fight. Nothing is searched and no time passes.
- **No ambush while you read the result.** The time a hunt takes now passes when you close the result, not while it's on screen. Before, a wandering enemy could appear and walk up while you were still reading.
- A hunt is no longer offered while an enemy is still on its way in.

## Ships
- **No fishing prompt at the helm.** At sea, the "Open Water / Cast the net" prompt sat in the middle of the screen while you steered, and got in the way when you aimed the bow guns. It no longer appears while you're at the helm, aiming the guns or boarding. You can still fish from a deck you're standing on.
- **The ship's sound no longer cuts out.** Taking the helm and getting under way, or slowing down, used to restart the boat's sound from the beginning, so it cut out. The sound now carries on smoothly as you speed up and slow down.
- **No more silent boats.** With more than one boat nearby, one could be left silent until it started moving. Every boat can be heard now.
