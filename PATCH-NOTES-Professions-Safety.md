# Patch Notes: Professions Safety

## Saving
- Online, your character is now **saved straight away** after you take materials out of the Stores, craft something,
  pay a smith, buy, collect, list or take back a piece at the market, or sell Marks at the Bank. A crash right after no
  longer loses them.

## Deleting an online character
- **Its professions and their Stores are deleted with it**, like its Renown, its home and its guild place. The delete
  window says so.
- A character with **market business still open** can't be deleted yet: a listing, an auction, a leading bid, a buy
  order, a commission, or goods on the way or waiting to be collected. Settle it first. Marks held for a bid or an
  order always go back to your account, never to one character.
