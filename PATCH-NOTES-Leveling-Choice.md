# Patch Notes: The leveling choice

## A new look for the leveling question
- **The "How will you grow?" screen now uses the Enhanced Plus look.** After you make a character, the choice between Daggerfall and Oblivion Remastered leveling opens in the same framed window as the rest of the Enhanced Plus interface, in your chosen Plus colour. The two systems sit side by side as tiles (stacked on a phone), each with a short explanation. The one you pick is highlighted in brass.
- Choose with a click, with the arrow keys and Enter, or by pressing 1 or 2, as before.
- The Classic interface keeps its original screen.

## Online characters level the Oblivion way
- **New online characters always use Oblivion Remastered leveling.** Daggerfall leveling is now for offline characters only. Online, the choice screen still shows it, marked "Offline characters only", so you know what you're getting before you start.
- **Existing online characters keep their leveling.** If your online character already levels the Daggerfall way, nothing changes for them.
- Characters imported from a classic Daggerfall save on the online page also use Oblivion Remastered leveling.
- **Bringing an offline character online** switches the online copy to Oblivion Remastered leveling. The Bring online question tells you before you confirm. Your level, skills and any level-up you've already earned carry over; the skill bar starts empty. Your offline character keeps Daggerfall leveling offline.
- Offline, nothing changes: you still choose either system (when the Oblivion Remastered Like Leveling mod is on).
