# Patch Notes: The Empire Forgives Its Loans

## Every online loan is forgiven
- **The Bank of the Empire has forgiven every loan.** The next time you load an online character, any loan it owes is
  cleared, and the game tells you how much was forgiven.
- **You keep the gold you borrowed.**
- If you had **defaulted** on a loan, that's forgiven too. The Empire will lend to you again, and your deposits are no
  longer taken to pay the old debt.
- Reputation lost from a past default isn't returned; it recovers over time as usual.
- This is a one-time amnesty. Loans taken from now on have to be repaid as normal.
- Offline characters are not affected.
