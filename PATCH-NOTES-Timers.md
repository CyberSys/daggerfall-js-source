# Patch Notes: Timers

## The hourglass
- **A new Timers window, online.** An hourglass now sits next to your profile icon on the pause menu. Press it to see
  every shared event in the world with a live countdown:
  - **Oblivion Gates** - when the next one opens, and while one stands, when it seals and when it collapses
  - **Town raids** - each raid of the day, when it starts and when the raiders withdraw
  - **The Sunday Turning** - the seat week's settlement at Sunday 18:00 UTC, and the Reckoning on Friday 18:00 UTC
    when pledges lock
  - **This week's battles** - every scheduled siege, tourney and revolt, with who is fighting whom, and the Royal
    Tourney while one runs
  - **The Season's end**, when one is under way
  - **The resets** - the next game day (new bounty hunts and raids) and the daily reset at 00:00 UTC
- Events running now are listed first with the time until they end; everything else follows, soonest first.
- Times are shown in your own clock, and the countdowns tick live while the window is open.
- Tap outside the window or press Escape to close it and return to the pause menu.
