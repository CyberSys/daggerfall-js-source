# Patch Notes: Gathering on the crosshair, pickups at the centre

## Professions: choose from the loot list
- **Herb patches, ore veins, boulders, trees, bodies and fishing water now use the loot list.** Look at one and the same box that lists a body's loot shows its name, your profession, and what you can do there: "Pick Red Rose", "Quarry the stone", "Skin the Grizzly Bear", "Search the Grizzly Bear", and so on.
- **Pick an action the way you take loot.** Click it, press your activate key, or tap it. The Up arrow moves to the next action; you can rebind it in Controls.
- **An action you can't do yet says why**, right on its row: no tool, rank too low, or the day's limit reached.
- Starting a hold action (kneeling, steadying, skinning) from the list holds it for you, the same as using the tool from the hotbar.
- On the Classic skin and on touch screens, where the loot box isn't shown, the same choices come up as a small list window.

## Gathering is drawn on the crosshair
The gathering minigames no longer open a panel over the screen. Each one is drawn on and around your crosshair, so you can see the world while you work. The rules and timings haven't changed.
- **Mining:** the rock's five strike points are marked where they are on the rock and move as you look around. The glinting point shows the circle a blow must land in to count double. A blow flashes on the crosshair, gold when it hits the glint.
- **Logging:** a ring closes on the notch around your crosshair. It lights up in the band where a strike is a Clean Cut.
- **Herbs:** your hold fills as an arc around the crosshair. It turns orange if you bruise the plant by moving.
- **The Basket:** the glint appears near the crosshair with a ring showing how long it has left. Your three finds show as small diamonds.
- **Skinning:** the cutting line is laid on the body itself, with a faint band showing how far off it you can stray. The first point shows where to start, and points light up as you pass them.
- **Fishing:** winding the net fills an arc, with the throw distance beside it. While you wait, a float sits under the crosshair, and a gold ring flashes at a tug. Hauling is a small upright bar beside the crosshair; keep the weight inside the band.
- **One line of help.** A short hint sits under the crosshair and fades after a couple of seconds. Your progress shows as small diamonds above it.
- Every strike, find, slip, splash and tug also has a sound.
- With reduced motion turned on in your system, nothing pulses. Logging uses a short still bar instead of the closing ring.

## Crafting stations
- **The anvil's heat, the loom's beat and the workbench's plane use the Enhanced Plus look:** the same dark stone frame as the loot box, with your strikes shown as diamonds. They work exactly as before.

## Pickups at the centre of the screen
- **On the Enhanced skin, what you pick up shows at the centre of the screen.** Each item gets a card with its picture, its name in its rarity colour, and how many you took. Gold shows as "+N Gold".
- Take more of the same item while its card is up and the count goes up instead of adding another card.
- Up to four cards show at a time. Each stays about two and a half seconds, then fades. They stay clear of the crosshair, the loot box, the hotbar and the HUD.
- Works for every quick-loot take, one item or Take All, from a body, a dropped pile or a catch of fish.
- If something can't be taken (your pack is too heavy, for example), you're still told why.
- The Classic skin keeps Daggerfall's own pickup message.

## World of Daggerfall: bushes on the ground
- **The bushes around bandit camps and other World of Daggerfall sites no longer float in the air or sink into the hillside.** Each one now stands on the ground under it. Tents, fires, walls and rocks are where they were.

## Party travel: the ready-up stays on screen
- **The leader keeps the Party tab while the party readies up.** Choosing a destination on the travel map used to close the Party tab, taking the ready count and Call off with it. It now comes back when the map closes.
- The leader's "Waiting for the party to ready up (n/m ready)" message stays up for the whole round and updates as people answer.
- A member who has the Party tab open answers there with Ready or Stay behind. The pop-up question only appears if the tab is closed.
