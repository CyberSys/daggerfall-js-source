# Patch Notes: Audit 29 - the Professions

An audit of Herbalism, Mining, the Stores, the Forge and Court writs, with every problem it found fixed.

## Gathering
- **E** goes to a herb patch or a vein only when you are looking right at it, it is within reach, and nothing stands between you. A door, a chest or a body beside a node now gets your press.
- A node you cannot work yet (the wrong rank, no tool) no longer takes **E**: the prompt still says what it needs, and the press goes on to what you meant.
- In a dungeon, only **E** starts mining a vein. A click or a tap no longer starts it by accident, and your weapon is never held back because of it.
- **Escape** ends a gathering act underground without also opening the pause menu.
- Holding the attack button into an act no longer keeps swinging afterwards, and a readied spell is no longer cast by a strike.
- Walking over a map square's edge while mining no longer ends the act.
- The Basket's search can no longer be done by mashing the attack button: a press before a glint shows misses it.
- Veins in dungeons no longer stand in doorways, and never float over a pit.
- Herb patches keep off camps, shrines and ruins, as the trees and grass do.
- A vein never stands inside a rock.

## The Stores and the Forge
- The Professions and Stores pages no longer re-ask the server in a loop when it cannot be reached (this could freeze the game).
- Signing into another account shows that account's Stores at once.
- A withdrawal whose answer was lost is sent again when you open the Stores page, and is never minted twice with two tabs open.
- The smith is paid for a smelt whose first answer was lost.
- The **Forge** home station is offered only where it works: online, while the professions are open to you, on the Enhanced interface. A Forge that cannot work says so.
- Motherlode Sense can no longer be chosen before the Motherlodes exist.
- Changing a specialisation you thought was free (because it was chosen on another device) is refused instead of charging 1,000 Marks.

## Mining and Court writs
- On confirmed land, each kingdom's own ore stands beside the usual veins instead of replacing them, so every rank still finds ore there.
- The XP shown after a harvest or a smelt is the XP you actually gained.
- Smelting low-tier metal at a high Smithing rank gives a quarter of the XP, as gathering does.
- The day's first Court writ always asks for the highest tier on offer.
- A writ you already filled, taken again after a reload, now says it is yours.

## Rollout
- The professions stay open to the developers first. Offline, nothing changes.
