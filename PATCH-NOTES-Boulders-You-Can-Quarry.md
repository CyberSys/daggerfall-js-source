# Patch Notes: Boulders you can quarry

## Professions
- **More boulders, and at the rock.** A rock field's boulders mostly never appeared, and the few that did often had their loose stones out on open ground, well away from any rock. Most boulders now appear each day, each with its stones at the foot of a rock you can see. Ore veins sit at the rocks more often too.
- **The prompt now says when you can't gather because you're in a settlement.** Mining, quarrying, herb gathering and logging don't work in a town, farm, temple, tavern or wealthy home, or within a city block of one. Before, a vein, boulder, tree or herb patch there showed as ready, and E or the tool then said "You cannot mine in a settlement!". Now the prompt says "not in a settlement" before you try. Dungeon veins aren't affected.

## Good to know
- Only a few of a rock field's boulders can be quarried each day. The ones you can work glow and show on the compass. Walk up to one until the prompt shows, then press **E** or use the Pick-Axe.
