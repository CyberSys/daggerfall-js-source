# Patch Notes: Boulders you can quarry

## Professions
- **More boulders, and at the rock.** A rock field's boulders mostly never appeared, and the few that did often had their loose stones out on open ground, well away from any rock. Now each boulder's stones sit at the foot of a rock you can see, and a large rock can hold more than one boulder or vein on its different sides.
- **More boulders each day.** Each map square now holds up to 3 boulders a day in woodlands, haunted woodlands and subtropical land (was 1), 4 in mountain woods (was 2), and 5 in mountains and deserts (was 3). Swamps and rainforests still have none. Ore veins sit at the rocks more often too.
- **Settlements.** Mining, quarrying, herb gathering and logging don't work in a town, farm, temple, tavern or wealthy home, or within a city block of one. Veins, boulders, herb patches and trees no longer appear there, so nothing glows or shows on the compass that you can't work. At the edge of that area, the prompt now says "not in a settlement" rather than showing a ready prompt that E then refuses. Dungeon veins aren't affected.
- **Fishing: cast at any angle.** The net's prompt only showed while you looked level or down at the water. It now shows whether you look down, level or up, up to about 55 degrees either way. An herb patch or other spot you look at directly still comes first.

## Good to know
- The boulders you can quarry glow and show on the compass. Walk up to one until the prompt shows, then press **E** or use the Pick-Axe.
