# Patch Notes: The pause key, the fatigue death loop and shared quests

## Fixes
- **Pause key:** if you moved Pause off Escape (even to a Ctrl or Alt combo), the same key now closes the pause screen. It closes when you let go, and holding it no longer flickers it. Holding F9 saves once, and a held key no longer reopens a window it just closed.
- **Arrest on fast travel:** online, a guard's hit as you fast travel no longer locks the game in an empty courtroom. Loading a save mid-arrest no longer carries the arrest into it.
- **Shop confirm box:** wear bars, runes and padlocks no longer draw over the Yes/No box.
- **Speed:** your Speed now sets your first-person swing and bow cooldown, and the third-person swing keeps the same pace.
- **Fatigue death loop (online):** you get up with at least as much fatigue as health, and time on the death screen no longer drains you, worsens a disease or rolls ambushes the moment you stand.
- **Criminal Conspiracy:** online, a bad reputation still drifts back toward zero every 112 game days while you're away. Good standing is never lost to time away.
- **Swimming:** swimming up into a dungeon ceiling or along a wall no longer pops you out of the level.
- **Flying monsters:** flyers, like the Dragonslayer quest's dragonling, no longer spawn half in the floor.
- **Shared quests:** when the friend who shared a quest finishes it, it completes for everyone, rewards paid once, even if it ends inside a building or dungeon. The quest's house opens for everyone on it, and The Courier gives each of you a package.
- **Party quest monsters:** a partner's quest monster counts for your quest only if they shared it with you, and one handed on when its owner leaves isn't lost.
- **Quest houses:** a house picked again by a later quest shows the new quest's name, on the town map too. Your own house and your map notes keep their names.
