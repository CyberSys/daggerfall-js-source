# Patch Notes: Real forests

## The wilderness
- **Trees grow together in forests.** Trees used to be spread evenly over the whole map, one every few steps, all lined up in rows. Now they gather into woods, from small copses to forests a few kilometres across, with open grassland between them dotted with bushes, flowers, rocks and the odd lone tree.
- **Forests run on from one area to the next.** A forest doesn't stop where the land around you finishes loading.
- **No more rows.** Every tree and bush stands at its own spot, so a wood looks like a wood.
- **Dungeons hide in the woods.** Dungeons, ruins, shrines, graveyards, covens and World of Daggerfall's camps, forts and ruins are usually surrounded by forest, with a clearing round them big enough for their tents and walls. Towns, farms and taverns stand in cleared fields. World of Daggerfall's rock fields and mountains are scenery: the woods grow round them as anywhere else, but never through the rocks.
- **Paths stay clear.** No tree or bush grows on a road or a track.
- **The Oblivion Gate keeps its clearing.** No tree stands through the gate, its plinth or the Sigil Broker.
- **Deserts are unchanged.** The Desert and Subtropical lands keep Daggerfall's own scattered plants.
- **Loggers chop in the woods.** The day's trees to fell stand in the forests whenever there's a forest nearby. The lone trees of open land are only used where there's no wood at all.

**Real forests** is under *The world* in Features. It's on by default and takes effect when the world next loads. Turn it off to spread the trees evenly, as Daggerfall does.

Forests hold more trees than Daggerfall's even scatter, about two and a half times as many on average. If the outdoors runs slowly on your machine, turning Real forests off is one more way to lighten it.

## Online note
- Online, Real forests is on for everyone, so everyone in a room walks the same woods and chops the same trees.
- If you have the game open from before this update, refresh the page so your woods match everyone else's.
