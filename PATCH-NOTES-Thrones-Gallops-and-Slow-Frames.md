# Patch Notes: Thrones, Gallops and Slow Frames

## Fixes
- **Walk-on switches work:** standing on something that triggers when walked on now counts wherever you stand on it, as in Daggerfall. The throne puzzle opens, and walk-on plaques and trap rooms that never fired now do. Trapped stairs bite on the way up and down as they should.
- **Castle floors stay quiet:** walking across a castle floor no longer counts as bumping into it. In Orsinium this set off the trespass warning and turned the whole castle hostile.
- **The gallop stops at the door:** riding into a building no longer leaves the hoofbeats playing inside.
- **Brigandine, fur and mail stay in the name:** a Magic or Rare piece of Roleplay & Realism armour keeps its "Brigandine", "Fur" or "Mail", so it is clear why a class that cannot wear leather is refused a brigandine helmet. Pieces you already have are renamed when you load, including those kept in chests, dropped on the floor or stowed on a boat.
- **Footsteps:** brigandine and fur armour now sound like leather, and mail like mail.
- **Slow frames no longer rebuild the screen:** when the game ran slowly, message boxes, notices, the name plaque and other panels were rebuilt every frame, which made it slower still. They stay put now.
- **Info mode only looks:** looking at a quest character in Info mode no longer touches them. In the Mages Guild's guard quest, touching the sleeping mage is a trap.
- **Steady lamplight indoors:** people's shadows no longer flicker as you turn the camera (worst in the Mages Guild). Each shadow faces its own lamp, and people standing idle keep their shadow on every lamp. A monster or player who stops and walks on leaves no ghost of their shadow behind, and your own shadow falls from the lamps nearest you, even with the camera pulled back.
- **Swing speed is sane again:** Speed still quickens your swing, on a gentler curve, and the weapon counts: daggers and fists are quick, heavy two-handers slow unless you're strong. Nothing swings much past twice a second (fast characters and werewolves were at four). In third person your character swings once per blow (a werewolf clawed two or three times).

## Good to know
- **Fatigue stuck at 1%** means you are carrying more than 75% of what you can (Roleplay & Realism's overload). Drop some weight and the drain stops.
- **Light through walls** with Enhanced Lighting on comes from carried lights (a torch, a lantern, a Light spell), which cast no shadows.
- **The FPS counter** now shows script time in dungeons and interiors too.
