# Patch Notes: Oblivion Gates - healing on the damage chart

Healers now get credit. The damage chart that shows after the Warden falls has a **Healed** column.

## Healed
- Each challenger's row shows how much they healed other challengers during the fight: healing spells cast on a party member, or on another player who has **Spells from strangers** turned on. A heal you cast on someone else counts as yours.
- Healing yourself doesn't count. Your own potions, spells and items keep you standing, but they don't show here.
- Only health that actually came back counts. Healing past a full health bar doesn't count.
- The column shows whenever anyone in the court healed someone else. On Legion-Lord nights it comes after the Host column. On a phone in portrait it takes the place of the Share column.
- Healing is shown on the chart only. It doesn't change your part in the fight or your spoils.
- The relay keeps the numbers honest: how much healing each challenger can take is capped by their level.

Reload the game after the update to have your healing counted.
