# Patch Notes: Professions - Smithing, Quality and the Anvil

## Smithing (online)
- The **Stores page** (on the Enhanced pause menu) has an **Anvil** section beside the Forge. Stand inside a **Weaponsmith's** or an **Armorer's** (50 gold a craft), or your own home with a **Forge** station, and make arms and armour from your Stores' ingots.
- **Weapons** (Dagger to Dai-katana, Mace, Flail, Warhammer and the axes), **plate** (Cuirass, Greaves, Helm, Pauldrons, Gauntlets, Boots) and **shields** (Buckler to Tower Shield) can be made in every metal: Iron, Steel, Silver, Moonstone (Elven), Dwarven, Mithril, Adamantium, Ebony, Orichalcum (Orcish), Daedric and Warforged Steel. Each needs your Smithing rank for its metal.
- **Chain** armour is made from Steel, for three quarters of plate's ingots.
- **Tools** - the Wood-Axe, Pick-Axe, Sickle and Spade - are made from Iron.
- **Repair Kits** are made in every metal but Warforged Steel.
- Some recipes also want Cured Leather or Oak or Pine planks. Until Hunting and Logging arrive, a smith sells these and Charcoal from their **stock** for Marks (Leather and Oak 4, Pine and Charcoal 2). They go into your Stores and are used there - they cannot be withdrawn.

## The heat
- **Craft** puts the ingot in the fire. Its glow rises and falls; **strike** (the Strike button, Space or Enter) three times while it is inside the band. Better Strength and Agility widen the band.
- Three strikes in the band is a **clean** craft, one quality step better. **Quick craft** skips the heat. Let it cool and nothing is lost.

## Quality
- Every piece is **Crude**, **Standard**, **Fine**, **Superior** or **Masterwork**. The further your rank is above the recipe's, the better the odds.
- A clean heat, your specialisation (Weaponsmith for weapons, Armoursmith for armour) and a Warforged Steel ingot each add a step. Nothing goes past Masterwork.
- Crude pieces wear out sooner. Fine pieces last longer and are lighter. Superior and Masterwork pieces last longer still, are lighter again, and come with an enchantment as loot does - Superior a Magic one, Masterwork a Rare one.
- A **Masterwork** carries its maker's name: "Aldric's Mithril Longsword".
- A tool's quality is its life: 37 uses Crude, up to 65 for Superior and Masterwork.
- Every piece shows its quality and its maker, and has a provenance record kept by the server.

## Repair Kits
- Use a Repair Kit from your pack and it mends the most-worn weapon or armour of its metal by a quarter of its condition, never past whole. A Steel kit mends chain too. The kit is used up; if nothing of its metal needs mending, it is kept.

## Specialisations
- **Weaponsmith** and **Armoursmith** (rank 50) now add their quality step.
- **Masterwright** (rank 100): Masterwork is 5% more likely.
- **Quartermaster** (rank 100): each unit smelted makes two ingots, and each Repair Kit craft makes two kits.

## Experience
- A craft gives 20 Smithing XP a tier of its metal, and 500 more the first time you make that recipe. Recipes well below your rank give a quarter.

## Fixes
- A Quartermaster's smelting gave one ingot a unit; it now gives two.

## Rollout
- The professions open to the developers first. Everyone else's game is unchanged until they are switched on for all.
- Offline, nothing changes. A piece made online is an ordinary item in your pack anywhere.
