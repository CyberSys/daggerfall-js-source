# Patch Notes: Sigil Sets

Armour sets with set builds, online only.

## The sets
Wear 2, 4 or 6 pieces of one set (body armour, a shield, one weapon):
- **Malacath's Bulwark** (tank): armour and Endurance; foes take back part of each blow; **Unbroken**: a killing blow leaves you at 1 health, damage halved for a few seconds.
- **Dagon's Brand** (melee): Strength and Critical Strike; **Bloodfury**, stronger below half health; **Rampage**: kills stack weapon damage.
- **Nocturnal's Shroud** (stealth, archery): Stealth and Agility; **Nightfall Strike** on foes that haven't noticed you; **Eventide**: a kill cloaks you in Chameleon.
- **Mora's Mantle** (magic): Intelligence and every school; cheaper spells; **Eye of Mora** absorbs Destruction spells.

## Growing together
- Magic-or-better armour, shields and weapons you win online can carry a set's sigil.
- Every worn piece grows with your Renown XP. A set runs Faint to Ascendant at its **least-grown** piece, capped by your Renown.
- Sets sleep offline and in duels.

## Aetheric: the boss set
- A new rarity above Legendary. Each Valkynaz Ruhn kill has a 1 in 6 chance to drop you a piece of **Ruhn's Regalia** (nine Daedric pieces).
- **The Burning Gate** (fire resistance, searing blows), **Cleave**, and **Wrath of the Warden**: a Flame Nova when a blow takes you below 30% health.

## The Sigil Broker
- A Daedra trader stands beside each Oblivion Gate while it stands.
- Spend **Sigil Stones** on the day's stock: a piece of each set, a set weapon and a piece of the Regalia. Same stock for everyone, new each day at midnight UTC. One of each per character per day.
- Locked stones are never spent.

## On screen
- Item cards show the set, the pieces you wear, its stage and all three tiers.
- The character screen lists your worn sets; set pieces wear their set's rune.
- Active and recovering set powers show on the HUD.
- The Oblivion Gate's name and countdown now show under the crosshair.
