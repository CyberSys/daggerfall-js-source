# Patch Notes: The way there

## Quests (Enhanced interface)
- **Your journal now says where a quest points.** Under each active quest, in the Quests tab and in the chronicle (the L key), a line names the place its latest journal entry sends you, in the same words Daggerfall uses: "Llugwych in Wayrest province". It only names what the entry itself says, or what is already on your map: a quest that says "a house in Daggerfall" still leaves the house for you to find.
- **Show on map.** Beside that line, a button closes the journal and opens the travel map on the place, as Daggerfall's own logbook always could with a click. It appears on the road, where the map can open. Indoors, in a dungeon or with an enemy near, the map refuses as it always has and tells you why.
- **"Not on your map yet."** If the place is named but not yet on your map, the journal tells you to ask around for directions. Townsfolk can mark it for you.
- **The chronicle shows how long you have left.** A quest with a deadline now shows "Time remains" on its card, counting down live and turning gold under a day.
- Older journal entries that named a different place keep their own Show on map in the Quests tab.
- The Classic logbook is unchanged.
