# Patch Notes: A cursor for the Basket

## Herbalism
- **The mouse cursor now appears for the Basket's search.** The glints show around the crosshair, not on it. Before, moving the mouse toward one turned your view, and the glint moved with it, so you couldn't click it. Now the cursor is free while you search, and your view stays still. Click a glint while it shows to find it. When the search ends, however it ends, the mouse goes back to looking around.
- Clicking anywhere while a glint shows still counts, so your attack key, a controller's trigger and a tap on a phone work as before.

## The other gathering acts
- **Mining and Skinning take the look back.** You aim these with the crosshair: the rock's strike points and the cutting line on the body. If you had freed the mouse (Enter or the Free Mouse key) before you started, the look now comes back so you can aim.
- Logging, picking herbs by hand or with the Sickle, and Fishing don't use the mouse cursor, so they're unchanged. The anvil, loom, workbench and mason's bench already open with the cursor free.
