# Patch Notes: The Caged Broker

## Dagon's Breach
- **The Sigil Broker is now held prisoner by Dagon's Faithful.** She waits in a cage at the faithful's rite circle, near the breach, from the omen at 17:00.
- **Kill every one of the faithful, including the Summoner, to free her.** You have to do it before the breach opens at 20:00. When the last of them falls, her cage door swings open for everyone online.
- If someone else already killed the Summoner, killing the rest still frees her.
- You can talk to her from any side of the cage.
- If the faithful are still standing when the breach opens, they pass into it and she stays caged for the night.
- Once freed, she trades until midnight, even if the Warden falls early and the breach collapses.

## Behind the scenes
- Everyone online is disconnected once when this update goes live, and reconnects.
