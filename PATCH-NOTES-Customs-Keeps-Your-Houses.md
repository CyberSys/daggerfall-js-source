# Patch Notes: Customs keeps your houses

## Your houses, ship and furniture come with you
- **Bring online keeps every house, ship and piece of furniture.** Customs used to leave houses and ships behind (with the furniture you bought for them) when everything you owned came to more than your level's allowance. Now they always come with the character.
- The realm's bank **won't buy back** a house or ship that came through customs, and furniture that came with it gives no gold back when you take it out. You keep them to live in and use.
- Gold over your level's allowance still stays behind, as before. Your offline character keeps everything and keeps playing offline.

## Getting back what customs kept
- **If customs kept back your house, ship or furniture, it comes back.** Play that online character in the browser or app that still has its offline character, and it's there, with every piece in its place. You'll be told what came back.
- Nothing is taken to make room. If you've since bought another house in that region, or another ship, you keep that one, and the old one stays with your offline character.

## "Never saved" characters
- If a character you brought online shows **Never saved**, its first save didn't land. Its tile now says **Undo bringing in**. It gives back everything that came with it: your online home, its furniture, your guild place, and the character's one trip through customs. Or press **Bring online** on it again to finish.
- Deleting one no longer takes its home and furniture with it.
- **Online homes lost this way before this update are being restored.** They come back to your offline character; bring it online again and they come with it.

## Behind the scenes
- While those homes are restored, online saving pauses once for about a minute. Your game waits and saves when it's back.
