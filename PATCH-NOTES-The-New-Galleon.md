# Patch Notes: The new galleon

## Ships
- **A new galleon.** The Small Ship - the galleon you can buy, and the hull the pirate brigantines, merchant galleons and navy cutters sail - is a brand-new model, built by Mac and fitted out from stem to stern.
- **Doors and hatches that open and close.** Both hatch covers on her main deck lift open, and the door into her great cabin and the door in the bulkhead on her gun deck swing open and shut. Use them like any boat door. A ladder runs down under each hatch to her gun deck.
- **A wheel at the helm.** Her wheel stands on the castle roof in front of where you steer, and turns with the rudder as you put the helm over under sail. Under oars the wheel and rudder stay centred, as on the old galleon.
- **Sails and ropes.** She carries five sails: a fore course, two topsails, a big gaff sail on the main and a jib. They furl and set, fill with the wind and swing as you trim. Shrouds with ratlines, stays, backstays and running rigging hold them up.
- **Painted properly.** A painted hull with gilt bands, a panelled castle, a glazed stern gallery that glows at night, planked decks, gratings, red gunport shutters and tarred rigging.
- **Cannon fire from the gunports.** She has five gunports a side. When you lay a broadside, that side's shutters swing up and the guns run out through the ports. Each ball leaves from its own port, and each gun kicks back as it fires before it's hauled out again. Ships of hers at sea open their ports when they run their guns out.
- **Her castle is part of her deck.** Two staircases climb to the castle roof and the helm. Her crew and boarders can walk up them, so nobody at the wheel is out of reach. Her officer and coxswain keep their posts up there while the hands work the main deck.

## Update: Mac's newer model
- **The galleon is Mac's updated model.** Her hull runs deeper below the waterline, with a sharper bow and a forefoot that sweeps up to the stem.
- **Deck beams.** Six heavy oak beams now carry the main deck over the gun deck. The gun deck's lanterns hang from them.
- **64x64 textures.** Every texture on her is 64x64, the size of Daggerfall's own. The painted bands on her hull, castle and stern gallery still run unbroken round her.

## Update: the audit
- **Broadsides always leave through open ports.** A quick click now runs each gun out and swings its shutter up as it fires. Other players see your shutters open and your guns run out when you lay a broadside. Each ball leaves from its own port even while she heels.
- **The port side's shutters swing up and out** like the starboard side's, instead of into the gun deck. Every shutter now fits snugly against her hull and is solid, so you can't crawl out through a shut port.
- **Standing at the helm,** you now stand on the castle roof with a clear view over the wheel and binnacle. Before, you stood sunk into the great cabin below.
- **Her sails and rigging fit.** Square sails hang from their yards, the jib and stays no longer pass through the topsails, and every rope ends where it is made fast. Shots that pass through her sails now tear them wherever the canvas actually hangs, however you trim.
- **A cleaner hull.** Her shape now matches Mac's model as his own Blender draws it, every face cut on the same diagonals, side to side, and each face is lit as one smooth panel.
- **Small fixes:** the anchor no longer pokes into her bow, the stern lanterns stand on the rail, the hatch covers open flat onto the deck, clear of the big sail's boom, the rudder turns on the sternpost, the bed sits on the floor, the stove has its flue, and the crow's nest is capped.
- **Her deck.** Boarders can no longer drop through the port staircase into the great cabin, the crew stays on the main deck between jobs, nobody walks over an open hatchway, and standing on her gun deck counts as being aboard.
- **If her model fails to load,** the old galleon takes her place with its own guns, decks and sails.
- **Ships of hers** keep to water deep enough for her keel.

## Update: the second audit
- **Ships in harbour sail again.** Merchant galleons, navy cutters and pirate brigantines moored in a port used to stay at their berths for good, because the berths were too shallow for the new galleon's keel. Berths are now sounded for the deepest ship that uses them, so these ships put out and come back in. Where the sea floor shelves gently, berths lie a little further out from the quay than before.
- **If her model fails to load,** the old galleon now also sails in the shallower water it can reach, and the AI judges its guns by the old galleon's numbers.
- **Her ropes follow her sails.** Braces, sheets and the mainsheet now stay on their yards and boom as they swing. Before, they trailed behind by up to a couple of metres while the sails trimmed, and further through a gybe. When a sail is shot away, its sheets go with it.
- **Broadsides on the frame they fire.** A quick click's shutters are open on the very frame each ball leaves.
- **Other players' galleons.** When another player leaves the helm after a broadside, her guns run in and her ports shut on your screen; they used to stay out. Her laid broadside no longer flickers while you sail at a faster time scale, and her shots and flashes leave the ports you see as she sails. Switching the Naval arc off sets every galleon's guns at rest.
- **Shots tear only canvas that's there.** Chain shot passes through where a furled or shot-away sail would hang. Battle damage now takes her highest canvas first: main topsail, fore topsail, jib, gaff, then the fore course.
- **Sea ships' sails fill the right way.** A gaff, jib or lateen sail on a ship at sea now bellies away from the wind, on either tack. Before, they always bellied to starboard.
- **Boarders on the Carrack and through doors.** Boarders can cross the Carrack's shut cargo doors, and standing on them counts as being aboard. Boarders follow you through open doors into cabins, the new galleon's great cabin and the Carrack's rooms. Bodies on the Carrack no longer drop through her forecastle stair or get lifted onto her forecastle.
- **Smaller fixes:** her helm can be taken from in front of the wheel; her doors swing clear of their frames; her pictures are painted while the game loads instead of the first time a galleon comes into view, and her model loads alongside the other ships and is built only once; in Retro Mode she's drawn like every other ship.

## Balance
- The new galleon carries five guns a side instead of the old one's six, so ships built on this hull hit a little lighter in a broadside. A cautious pirate brigantine is less eager to attack a boat on its own.
- That lighter broadside changes who picks a fight at sea. A cautious pirate brigantine no longer goes after merchant carracks, so from level 5 the brigantines' raids are on merchant galleons only. A cautious corsair galley now attacks merchant galleons, so from level 7 you can come across those fights. A cautious brigantine now runs from a navy war galley, and from a crewed Large Galley of yours.

## Fixes
- Standing in the water or on a quay right next to a moored galleon no longer counts as being aboard her.
- A pirate boarding a prize that lies close to land now comes alongside instead of circling off.
- Head to wind with her sails set, her gaff and jib are taken aback and drive her astern: about 4 m/s in a steady wind, nearly half her speed ahead. The helm now tells you you're in irons while she goes astern. Put the helm over or strike sail to come out of it.
